# Lead-Gen via Meta & Google Ads für Ästhetische Medizin & Schönheitschirurgie in Deutschland (Botox/Hyaluron, Fadenlifting/Skinbooster/Profhilo, Brust-OP/Fettabsaugung/Lidstraffung/Nasen-OP, Haartransplantation)

Research date: 2026-11-20. Method note: Only WebSearch worked in this session. WebFetch was blocked for every host tried (dgaepc.de, drweb.de, thelocal.de, mww-consulting.de, isaps.org could not be reached because they are outside the session's network allowlist). **The Meta Ad Library and live Google SERPs could not be inspected directly.** Most findings below therefore come from search-result snippets and summaries, not full-text reads. Figures should be checked against the original pages before they go into a client-facing document.

## 1. Which ads run in Germany (Meta Ad Library / Google), and what patterns recur?

### Takeaway
The Meta Ad Library itself could not be opened. What the indirect evidence shows: the low-ticket injectables segment (Botox, lip filler) runs on aggressive "ab X €" entry prices, such as Botox from 129 € and lip filler from 99–139 €/ml. These offers come from chains, booking platforms and also from non-physicians on Kleinanzeigen. Before/after creatives, the strongest format internationally, are now clearly illegal in Germany for Hyaluron and OPs (BGH 2025), and the ban is actively enforced through Abmahnungen.

### Cited Findings
- **Price-anchor offers (Berlin/Hamburg):** a Berlin blog/ranking lists "botox günstig berlin ab 129 €" and "ab 139 €" (blog post about 300 days old, itself advertising) — [Genclique Blog](https://www.genclique.com/post/botox-gunstig-berlin). A Berlin location of the "MY health and beauty" concept advertises a "Lip Flip mit Botox ab 149,99 €" — [MY Gesundbrunnen-Center Berlin](https://myhealthandbeauty.app/pages/location-berlin-gesundbrunnencenter). A Berlin practice advertises lip filler "1 ml ab 139 € – nur für kurze Zeit" (undated) — [B1 Medical Berlin](https://b1-medical.de/berlin/).
- **Booking/comparison platforms aggregate offers:** injectablesbooking.de runs a dedicated page of "Botox-, Filler- & Profhilo-Angebote". One listed example is a Kalia Lab location in Hamburg with "Botox Zone ab 229 €" and "Filler pro ml ab 379 €" — [injectablesbooking.de/angebote](https://injectablesbooking.de/angebote).
- **Grey market on Kleinanzeigen:** Hyaluron lip treatments in Hamburg/Berlin are offered there with 2026 dates. One listing advertises an "Aktionspreis 99€/ml" — [Kleinanzeigen Hamburg Lippenunterspritzung](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-hamburg/lippenunterspritzung/k0l9409); [Kleinanzeigen Berlin Hyaluron Lippen](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-berlin/hyaluron-lippen/k0l3331).
- **Doctor-influencer model / before-after:** The "Dr. Rick & Dr. Nick" brand (Aesthetify GmbH) advertised Hyaluron treatments on Instagram with before/after images. Verbraucherzentrale NRW issued an Abmahnung, then sued, and the BGH confirmed the ban on 31.07.2025 (I ZR 170/24) — [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtsprechung/bgh-izr17024-influencer-aesthetify-vergleichsbilder-werbung-hyaluron-dr-rick-dr-nick-2025-07-31); [Verbraucherzentrale – Unterlassungsklage gegen Aesthetify](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.de/verbandsklagen/unterlassungsklage-gegen-die-aesthetify-gmbh-94286); [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/izr17024-bgh-vorher-nachher-bildern-schoenheitsoperation-dr-rick-und-nick-aesthetify).
- **Story sequences count as before/after too:** OLG Frankfurt held that Instagram story sequences can be prohibited before/after advertising (reported 27.11.2025) — [Rödl & Partner](https://www.roedl.com/insights/olg-frankfurt-a-m-story-sequenzen-koennen-verbotene-vorher-nachher-werbung-sein/). Linking to before/after photos on Instagram is also unfair competition (LG Köln, 2021) — [Heuking](https://www.heuking.de/de/news-events/newsletter-fachbeitraege/artikel/lg-koeln-vorher-nachher-fotos-von-schoenheitsoperationen-unlauter-und-auch-auf-instagram-verboten.html).
- **Ärzteblatt 2026 warns about medical advertising on social media** ("Ärztliches Werberecht: Vorsicht auf Social Media", DÄ 2026, Heft 4) — [Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/archiv/titel/dae/2026/4/aerztliches-werberecht-vorsicht-auf-social-media-6eb5710d-c3c4-4c72-8951-e55e60f54bbb).
- **German agencies' recommended creatives:** Rise Digital advertises "HWG-konforme" Meta Ads with video shoots directly in the practice (i.e. doctor-to-camera and practice footage instead of results images) — [Rise Digital](https://rise-digital.de/). International agencies likewise recommend educational creatives, because before/after or transformation promises get ads rejected — [catomarketing (US)](https://www.catomarketing.com/post/meta-ads-for-cosmetic-surgeons-how-to-fill-your-consultation-calendar-in-2026).
- **Lead form vs landing page (international, US):** Meta Instant Forms give a lower CPL but need fast, consistent follow-up. Facebook leads book at roughly half the rate of Google leads — [prospyrmed (US)](https://prospyrmed.com/blog/post/paid-ad-roi-benchmarks-aesthetic-clinics); [bestppcfirm (US)](https://bestppcfirm.com/blog/medspa-ppc-cost-per-lead-benchmarks).
- **Brand name "Botox" in public advertising:** One German agency FAQ says advertising Botox by brand name to the general public is prohibited under § 10 HWG (prescription-only drug) and recommends review by a Fachanwalt or the Ärztekammer. This is a secondary source and needs checking — [dominikvonfalkenhausen.de](https://dominikvonfalkenhausen.de/wissen/welche-marketingagenturen-sind-auf-kampagnen-fuer-faltenunterspritzungen-und-botox-spezial).
- **Haartransplantation:** Turkish clinics run German-language landing pages with price comparisons and package prices, e.g. Dr. Serkan Aygin with a German site and "360 Grad Tour", and emrahcinik.com/de/preise — [drserkanaygin.com/de](https://www.drserkanaygin.com/de/haartransplantation/); [emrahcinik.com/de/preise](https://emrahcinik.com/de/preise/). German media/advertorials also run Türkei price comparisons for 2026 — [Hamburg Magazin](https://www.hamburg-magazin.de/artikel/haartransplantation-in-der-tuerkei-preis-leistungs-vergleich); [Mallorca Magazin 08/2026](https://www.mallorcamagazin.com/nachrichten/leben-und-stil/2026/08/05/155655/haartransplantation-turkei-kosten-2026-der-ehrliche-preisvergleich.html).

### Inferences
- The injectables ad market (Botox/lips) is visibly price-driven ("ab 99/129/139 €"). Chains, booking platforms and grey-market providers set price expectations. An owner-run Praxis competing on price alone competes against these anchors.
- In Germany, before/after is effectively out for Hyaluron and OPs. Meta officially allows it with 18+ targeting (see section 6), but the HWG is stricter. Creatives therefore have to work with doctor-to-camera, practice/process footage, patient testimonials without result comparisons, and education. That raises creative production effort for the agency.
- Likely recurring offer patterns (inferred from the price pages above and international practice, **not verified in the Ad Library**): "kostenlose Erstberatung", "ab X €" entry prices, limited-time Aktionspreise, Lip Flip as a cheap entry product, and package prices for Haartransplantation (Türkei, all-inclusive with hotel and transfer).

### Gaps
- **Meta Ad Library (DE) could not be accessed**, so there are no direct observations of ad runtimes (longevity), number of active advertisers, creative formats or CTAs. Recommendation: search the Ad Library manually for "Faltenbehandlung", "Lippen", "Hyaluron", "Brustvergrößerung", "Haartransplantation", "Fettabsaugung" and note active ads older than 3 months per advertiser.
- No live Google SERP/ad copy for "Botox [Stadt]" or "Brust OP Kosten" could be read.

## 2. Prices / ticket values in Germany and repeat purchase / CLV

### Takeaway
Injectables sit at roughly 130–600 € per visit. A single Botox zone (129–250 €) and 1 ml of lip filler (139–380 €) often fall **below the 300 € filter**, while full-face or multi-zone treatments reach 350–900 €. Botox repeats every 3–5 months. All surgical procedures are clearly ≥1,000 € (Brust-OP about 3,100–7,700 €). For Haartransplantation, German prices (about 6,000 € for 2,000 grafts) are roughly 55–60 % above the Türkei.

### Cited Findings
- **Botox:** a treatment session (about 20 min) costs 350–600 € (DrWeb industry article, number of zones not stated) — [drweb.de (snippet)](https://www.drweb.de/aesthetische-privatkliniken-geschaeftsmodell/). Entry prices per zone of about 129–250 € in Berlin/Hamburg — [Genclique](https://www.genclique.com/post/botox-gunstig-berlin); [injectablesbooking.de](https://injectablesbooking.de/angebote) (Kalia Lab Hamburg "Botox Zone ab 229 €"). Botox-Center Berlin publishes its own price page — [botox-center-berlin.de/preise](https://www.botox-center-berlin.de/preise.html) (content not read).
- **Hyaluron:** a classic Faltenunterspritzung costs 400–900 € — [drweb.de (snippet)](https://www.drweb.de/aesthetische-privatkliniken-geschaeftsmodell/). Frankfurt clinic "ab 395 € pro ml" — [Central Aesthetics Frankfurt](https://www.schoenheitschirurgie-ffm.de/en/?p=5476). Lips 1 ml: 350 € (Berlin practice), "ab 139 €" (Aktion), Kleinanzeigen 99 €/ml, Kalia Lab "Filler pro ml ab 379 €" — [B1 Medical](https://b1-medical.de/berlin/); [Kleinanzeigen](https://www.kleinanzeigen.de/s-hamburg/lippenunterspritzung/k0l9409); [injectablesbooking.de](https://injectablesbooking.de/angebote).
- **Durability → repeat frequency:** Botox lasts about 3–5 months, Hyaluron about 6–12 months — [drweb.de (snippet)](https://www.drweb.de/aesthetische-privatkliniken-geschaeftsmodell/).
- **Brustvergrößerung (DE):** a comparison portal lists German clinics at about 3,100–4,200 € entry prices, individual offers at 6,900–7,740 €, and an "average price August 2026: 4,036 €" (methodology unclear, probably entry prices) — [123.clinic Deutschland](https://www.123.clinic/de/klinik-suche/brustvergrosserung/deutschland). GKV pays only in exceptional cases — [SINIS Aesthetics PDF](https://sinis-aesthetics.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/SINIS-PDFs-Kosten-Brustvergroesserung.pdf).
- **Lidstraffung / Fettabsaugung (CH, as an indicator, not DE):** Oberlidstraffung beidseits 3,290 CHF, Fettabsaugung one region 3,890 CHF (Kantonsspital, price list 2024) — [stgag.ch](https://www.stgag.ch/ext/tx_docs/stgag_plastischechirurgie_preisliste_240124.pdf).
- **Nasenkorrektur (AT, public fee schedule, not DE):** simple about 2,615 €, complex about 3,843 € — [Land Steiermark](https://www.verwaltung.steiermark.at/cms/dokumente/12878404_74835225/6c3ec2b2/Festsetzung%20von%20Geb%C3%BChren%20f%C3%BCr%20tagesklinische%20Leistungen_Anhang%20A.pdf). Private German rhinoplasty prices are likely clearly higher (unverified).
- **Haartransplantation DE:** about 3–6 € per graft, about 6,000 € for 2,000 grafts. Portal figures of 4,500–10,000 USD, FUE 7,500–12,000 USD — [bookimed DE](https://de.bookimed.com/clinics/country=germany/procedure=hair-transplant/); [medihair](https://medihair.com/de/haartransplantation-tuerkei/).
- **Haartransplantation Türkei:** about 2,500–5,500 € all-inclusive package (2026), unshaven about 3,200–3,700 USD. One provider calculates 1.07 €/graft in the Türkei vs 2.93 €/graft in DE — [Mallorca Magazin 2026](https://www.mallorcamagazin.com/nachrichten/leben-und-stil/2026/08/05/155655/haartransplantation-turkei-kosten-2026-der-ehrliche-preisvergleich.html); [bookimed TR](https://de.bookimed.com/clinics/country=turkey/procedure=unshaven-hair-transplant); [Hamburg Magazin](https://www.hamburg-magazin.de/artikel/haartransplantation-in-der-tuerkei-preis-leistungs-vergleich).
- **US benchmark (international):** median med-spa ticket $216 per visit (Zenoti data). Target rebooking rate 55–65 % for established practices (AmSpa) — [optimantra / Zenoti benchmarks 2026 (US)](https://optimantra.com/blog/med-spa-benchmarks-2026-revenue-retention-utilization-rates); [Zenoti medspa trends 2026](https://zenoti.com/thecheckin/medspa-trends-2026).

### Inferences
- **Illustrative Botox CLV (estimate):** 350–450 € per visit × 2.5–3 visits/year ≈ 900–1,350 €/year. With 2–3 years of retention ≈ 2,000–4,000 € CLV. Botox thus clears the ≥300 € filter only via CLV or multi-zone treatments, not via a single-zone entry ticket.
- Surgical procedures (≥3,000 €) and Haartransplantation in DE (≥5,000 €) clearly meet the ≥1,000 € ideal. For one procedure, a retainer of 999–1,500 € equals 15–50 % of a single OP ticket.
- **Fadenlifting, Skinbooster, Profhilo:** no reliable German prices found. From general market knowledge (unverified), Profhilo is typically a session of a few hundred euros in a 2-session protocol, and Fadenlifting usually costs several hundred to over 1,000 €.

### Gaps
- No DE price lists for Fadenlifting, Profhilo/Skinbooster, Fettabsaugung, Lidstraffung or Nasen-OP could be read (fetch blocked).
- No German data on actual CLV or retention of Botox patients.

## 3. CPL, cost per appointment, show-up and close rates

### Takeaway
German data is scarce. One German agency reports about 35 €/inquiry for a Praxis (budget 1,500–2,000 €/month). Another reports 100 € per qualified inquiry after optimization, starting from 500 € before. US benchmarks show Botox CPL of $12–30 on Meta, surgical CPL of $40–150, and $160–500 per booked appointment. Generic med-spa no-show rates are low at about 4–5 % for existing patients, but ad leads show much higher no-show rates (up to 40 % in an anecdote).

### Cited Findings
- **DE (agency, self-reported):** MWW Consulting reports **35.40 € per qualified inquiry** in a campaign for an ästhetische Praxis (11 inquiries) with a monthly budget of 1,500–2,000 €. Target < 30 €/inquiry, "eine Zielgröße, kein Versprechen". Budget is paid directly to Meta from the Praxis's own ad account — [mww-consulting.de](https://mww-consulting.de/blog/werbung-aesthetische-praxis/) (snippet; full text not readable).
- **DE (agency, anecdote):** 1,000 clicks at 2.50 € (2,500 € budget) → 5 unqualified inquiries = **500 €/inquiry**. After changes, **100 € per qualified inquiry** — [dominikvonfalkenhausen.de](https://dominikvonfalkenhausen.de/wissen/welche-marketingagenturen-sind-auf-kampagnen-fuer-faltenunterspritzungen-und-botox-spezial).
- **US benchmark, Botox Meta:** $12–30 CPL — [bestppcfirm (US)](https://bestppcfirm.com/blog/medspa-ppc-cost-per-lead-benchmarks). US case study: $17 average CPL, 367 Botox leads in 4 months ($14 Lip Flip, $32 general Botox) — [SagaPixel case study (US)](https://sagapixel.com/?p=24942). Boca Raton: 37 leads for $426 ≈ $7 CPL — [web search snippet, agency case (US)](https://sagapixel.com/?p=24936).
- **US benchmark, surgery:** Meta $15–45 per consultation inquiry (catomarketing). Others report $40–120 CPL on Meta for surgical procedures, and Brustvergrößerung $60–150 per consultation lead via Google — [catomarketing (US)](https://www.catomarketing.com/post/meta-ads-for-cosmetic-surgeons-how-to-fill-your-consultation-calendar-in-2026); [medicalmarketingfirm (US)](https://medicalmarketingfirm.com/procedures/breast-augmentation-marketing/). Plastic surgery Meta case: $20 CPL, 64 leads/month — [search snippet, US agency case](https://sagapixel.com/?p=24936).
- **Cost per booked appointment (US):** Meta aesthetics leads $18–40, booked appointment **$160–500** — [prospyrmed (US)](https://prospyrmed.com/blog/post/paid-ad-roi-benchmarks-aesthetic-clinics).
- **Lead→appointment (US, contradictory):** Google leads 15–25 % book vs social 8–12 % (one agency). Another source gives Facebook 25–40 % vs Google 40–60 % — [prospyrmed (US)](https://prospyrmed.com/blog/post/paid-ad-roi-benchmarks-aesthetic-clinics); [bestppcfirm (US)](https://bestppcfirm.com/blog/medspa-ppc-cost-per-lead-benchmarks). HawkSEM/Kalologie: +117 % conversions at half the CPL via paid social (US) — [HawkSEM](https://hawksem.com/results/kalologie/).
- **No-show (US, mostly existing patients):** 5 % no-show, 14 % cancellation (Zenoti 2025 data, >30,000 businesses). Another evaluation gives 4 % — [optimantra (US)](https://optimantra.com/blog/med-spa-benchmarks-2026-revenue-retention-utilization-rates); [Zenoti](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/medspa-cancellation-rate). Vendor anecdote: consultation-to-booking rose from 35 % to 68 % and no-shows fell from 40 % to 12 % after process changes (US) — [search result cluster, vendor case](https://www.keragon.com/blog/med-spa-no-show-reduction).
- **Canada (PRP hair):** $1,000 budget → >100 leads at about $10 → 10 booked consultations, i.e. about 10 % lead→appointment — [scalehaven](https://scalehaven.io/blog/cosmetic-clinic-lead-generation/).
- **General Meta average (US, all industries 2025):** $27.66 CPL — [webtonic / search snippet](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/medical-spa-aesthetics-facebook-ads-statistics).
- **Messenger case NL (DACH-adjacent):** aesthetic clinic Amsterdam, 30-day case study (details not read) — [seekadu](https://www.seekadu.com/use-cases/aesthetic-clinic-messenger).

### Inferences
- **Illustrative DE funnel (estimate):** CPL 25–60 € (injectables, Meta Lead Form) → 20–35 % booked → 60–75 % show → 50–70 % close. That gives **about 100–300 € CAC per treated patient** for injectables. For surgery, CPL is probably 60–150 €, with lower close rates (consultation → OP often 20–40 %, estimate), so **CAC about 500–2,000 € per OP**, against a 3,000–8,000 € ticket.
- The agency model (retainer from the first won customer) is very tight for injectables-only clients. With 1,200 € retainer plus, for example, 1,500 € ad spend, the Praxis needs about 6–10 new Botox patients per month at 350–450 € first ticket to break even on first revenue alone. That only works if retention (CLV) is counted in.
- Speed-to-lead and setter capacity (calling within minutes, WhatsApp follow-up) are critical. Most owner-run practices lack them, so the agency would probably have to deliver them (or bring a setter).

### Gaps
- No reliable German show-up or close-rate data, and no reddit/LinkedIn reports with concrete DE CPLs (fetch blocked, search found only agency pages).
- No German CPC data for "Botox", "Brustvergrößerung" or "Haartransplantation". The general DE CPC is 0.50–4 €, and 8 €+ in competitive industries (estimate, no data basis) — [selbststaendigkeit.de](https://selbststaendigkeit.de/marketing/online-marketing/google-ads-kosten/); historical Statista CPC by industry (2021) — [Statista](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/1251712/umfrage/cost-per-click-cpc-bei-google-ads-in-deutschland).

## 4. Market size, demand, number of providers, competition, ownership structure

### Takeaway
Germany is a top market. ISAPS counts about 361,000 Botulinum treatments in DE for 2024. In the DGÄPC statistics, Botox (13.8 %), Oberlidstraffung (12.8 %) and Faltenunterspritzung (10.5 %) lead for 2025. About 1,151 Fachärzte für Plastische und Ästhetische Chirurgie (BÄK, end of 2025, column meaning uncertain) compete with dermatologists, fachfremde Ärzte, Zahnärzte, Heilpraktiker and chains. Chains (60+ centres) and booking platforms are pushing into the market.

### Cited Findings
- **DGÄPC Statistik 2025** (published 14.11.2025): Botulinum 13.8 %, Oberlidstraffung 12.8 %, Faltenunterspritzung 10.5 % (shares of treatments). Men 11.4 %. The DGÄPC calls for a legal Facharztvorbehalt — [DGÄPC Statistik 2025 PDF](https://www.dgaepc.de/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/DGAECP-Statistik-2025-Web.pdf) (via search snippet); [Presseportal](https://presseportal.de/pm/167299/6158359).
- **Absolute numbers 2024 (members' survey, probably VDÄPC/DGPRÄC):** Botulinum 34,671, Hyaluron 23,689, Blepharoplastik 6,051. Top 10 for 2025: Botox, Hyaluron, Blepharoplastik, Fettabsaugung, Brustvergrößerung — [Ärzteblatt "Wieder mehr Botox und Fillerbehandlungen"](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/153059/Plastische-und-Aesthetische-Chirurgie-Wieder-mehr-Botox-und-Fillerbehandlungen); [Ärzteblatt Top-5 Eingriffe 2024](https://aerztestellen.aerzteblatt.de/de/redaktion/die-top-5-eingriffe-der-aesthetisch-plastischen-chirurgie-2024). These figures cover only members' practices and are therefore far below total market volume.
- **ISAPS 2024 (DE):** about 361,210 Botulinum procedures in Germany (via Statista) — [Statista non-surgical DE](https://statista.com/statistics/587319/non-surgical-cosmetic-procedures-performed-in-germany-by-type); [Statista injectables DE](https://www.statista.com/statistics/587313/injectable-cosmetic-procedures-performed-in-germany-by-type). Global: 20.5 million non-surgical and 17.4 million surgical procedures, 7.8 million Botulinum treatments — [ISAPS 2024 survey (German version)](https://www.isaps.org/media/s5nndrqf/2024-global-survey-german.pdf); [AmSpa](https://www.americanmedspa.org/news/isaps-releases-2024-global-survey-results-ranks-surgical-and-nonsurgical-procedure-popularity/).
- **Trend contradiction:** DGÄPC 2022–2023 saw a clear decline in Faltenunterspritzungen and Botulinum. A 2024 evaluation reports rising demand again — [DGÄPC Statistik 2023](https://www.dgaepc.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/692202-Prospekt-DGACP-Statistik-2023-Webversion.pdf); [Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/nachrichten/153059/Plastische-und-Aesthetische-Chirurgie-Wieder-mehr-Botox-und-Fillerbehandlungen).
- **Number of Fachärzte for Plastische und Ästhetische Chirurgie:** 1,151 (31.12.2025), 1,057 (2024), 1,017 (2023). The exact column meaning (total vs berufstätig) is unclear — [BÄK Ärztestatistik 2025](https://www.bundesaerztekammer.de/fileadmin/user_upload/BAEK/Ueber_uns/Statistik/AErztestatistik_2025.pdf).
- **Chains:** a StepStone listing (company not named) mentions "knapp 60 eigene Fachzentren" in Germany and nine other countries, with sites in Berlin, München, Hannover, Konstanz, Karlsruhe, Hamburg, Regensburg, Leipzig, Nürnberg and Stuttgart — [StepStone](https://www.stepstone.de/job/12870908). Further concepts: Kalia Lab (Hamburg among others), MY health and beauty (shopping-centre locations), Keren Sierra Aesthetics store-in-store at Breuninger Stuttgart — [injectablesbooking.de](https://injectablesbooking.de/angebote); [myhealthandbeauty.app](https://myhealthandbeauty.app/pages/location-berlin-gesundbrunnencenter); [StepStone Ingolstadt result](https://www.stepstone.de/results/aesthetik/in-ingolstadt).
- **Business model:** private aesthetic clinics are described as highly profitable (self-pay, short treatment times) — [drweb.de (headline/snippet)](https://www.drweb.de/aesthetische-privatkliniken-geschaeftsmodell/).
- **Agency competition:** there are specialized DE/AT agencies for aesthetics marketing (MWW Consulting, Rise Digital, Dominik von Falkenhausen, digitalpraxis.at), plus at least 21 US agencies with a Meta focus on med spas — [mww-consulting.de](https://mww-consulting.de/meta-ads-arztpraxis/); [rise-digital.de](https://rise-digital.de/); [digitalpraxis.at](https://digitalpraxis.at/dermatologie-aesthetik-marketing/); [webtonic (US)](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/best-meta-ads-fb-ig-agencies-for-medical-spa-aesthetics).

### Inferences
- **Target-client pool (estimate):** about 1,000–1,150 plastische Chirurgen (many employed in hospitals, so fewer owner-run practices), plus several thousand Dermatologen, MKG-Chirurgen, Allgemeinmediziner and Zahnärzte with an aesthetics side line, plus Heilpraktiker (Hyaluron only), plus Haartransplantations-Praxen (a few dozen to about 100 in DE, unverified). Most single practices with 1–3 doctors are owner-run. Chains and investor-backed concepts are not a target (in-house marketing).
- The market is mature, and price-anchored at the injectables end. Surgery and Haartransplantation have higher tickets but face strong competition from abroad (Türkei, Tschechien) and existing specialist agencies.

### Gaps
- No reliable numbers for Heilpraktiker doing aesthetics, Ästhetik-Praxen overall, or Haartransplantations-Kliniken in DE.
- No Google search volumes (Keyword Planner/Semrush not accessible).
- No figure for the overall German market in euros.

## 5. Who may perform what (target pool)

### Takeaway
**Botox:** only doctors (including Zahnärzte within limits), because it is prescription-only. Heilpraktiker and Kosmetikerinnen are excluded, and LG Bochum 2026 handed a Heilpraktiker a suspended sentence. **Hyaluron:** doctors and Heilpraktiker (with Heilpraktikererlaubnis); **not Kosmetikerinnen** (OLG Karlsruhe), except the needle-free IRI system (VG Düsseldorf 2025). **OPs and Haartransplantation:** doctors only. The DGÄPC is calling for a Facharztvorbehalt and prescription-only status for Hyaluron, which would be a regulatory risk for Heilpraktiker clients.

### Cited Findings
- LG Bochum (Feb. 2026): suspended sentence for a Heilpraktiker who injected Botox; the treatment is "ein medizinischer Eingriff, der klar dem Arztvorbehalt unterliegt" — [Rödl & Partner, 27.02.2026](https://www.roedl.com/insights/botox-injektionen-durch-heilpraktiker-lg-bochum-verhaengt-bewaehrungsstrafe/).
- Kosmetikerinnen and Heilpraktiker may not inject Botox — [The Local, 18.09.2026](https://thelocal.de/20260918/who-can-legally-give-you-cosmetic-botox-and-fillers-in-germany).
- Heilpraktiker with an Erlaubnis under § 1 HeilprG may in principle perform Faltenunterspritzungen (VG Münster / OVG NRW 2011/2013). Hyaluron, unlike Botox, is not generally prescription-only — [ZWP online](https://epaper.zwp-online.info/epaper/5470/export-article/76); [DGPRÄC statement on Heilpraktiker 2022](https://www.dgpraec.de/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/2022-01-06-Stellungnahme-zu-Heilpraktikern_v3.pdf).
- OLG Karlsruhe: Kosmetikerin may not inject Hyaluron into wrinkles — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/kosmetikerin-darf-falten-nicht-mit-hyaluronsaeurehaltigen-mitteln-unterspritzen-heilkunde-heilpraktikergesetz); [Ärzte Zeitung](https://www.aerztezeitung.de/Wirtschaft/Kosmetikerin-darf-Falten-mit-Hyaluron-nicht-unterspritzen-275381.html). - Zahnärzte and Botox: older decision "kein Botox durch Zahnärzte" (2011) for cosmetic indications — [IWW](https://www.iww.de/zp/archiv/berufsrecht-kein-botox-durch-zahnaerzte-f8001). Bayerische decision 2024 on the Heilpraktiker/aesthetics topic — [gesetze-bayern.de](https://www.gesetze-bayern.de/Content/Pdf/Y-300-Z-BECKRS-B-2024-N-23888).

### Inferences
- Target clients with a Botox offer must be doctor-led practices. Heilpraktiker are only suitable for Hyaluron/Skinbooster, carry legal and reputation risk, and could lose their business model if Hyaluron becomes prescription-only.
- Kosmetikstudios are out as clients for injection offers, apart from needle-free devices (a grey area).

### Gaps
- Current status of the Zahnärzte/Botox question (aesthetic indications) and the content and outcome of 1 BvR 2656/25 were not verified.

## 6. Specific risks (ad bans, policies, HWG, Schnäppchen leads, no-shows, Türkei)

### Takeaway
The biggest risk in Germany is the HWG, not Meta. Before/after for Hyaluron and OPs is banned (BGH 2025, also in stories). Brand names of prescription drugs (Botox) must not be advertised to the public. Werbegaben/Gewinnspiele (§ 7 HWG) are problematic. Enforcement comes through Verbraucherzentrale and Wettbewerbszentrale Abmahnungen. Meta requires 18+ targeting and prohibits negative body-image wording. Price-anchored injectables ads attract Schnäppchen leads, and for Haartransplantation/OPs the Türkei undercuts by 55–60 %.

### Cited Findings
- **§ 11 HWG / BGH I ZR 170/24 (31.07.2025):** before/after advertising for Hyaluron injections is prohibited, because they count as "operative plastisch-chirurgische Eingriffe" — [Ärzteblatt](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/bundesgerichtshof-untersagt-werbung-fur-hyaluronspritzen-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern-9682baf7-8727-478a-9535-5bb619b29bbe); [ra-plutte](https://www.ra-plutte.de/bgh-hyaluron-vorher-nachher-werbung-verboten/); [ZDFheute](https://www.zdfheute.de/politik/bgh-urteil-werbung-fotos-schoenheitsop-100.html). Landesärztekammer Hessen 2024 article on advertising for Faltenunterspritzung — [LÄKH](https://www.laekh.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Heftarchiv/Einzelartikel/2024/10_2024/Recht_Werbung_Faltenunterspritzung.pdf).
- **§ 7 HWG (Werbegaben):** OLG Frankfurt treats the mere chance to take part in a Gewinnspiel as a Werbegabe. The Wettbewerbszentrale had a "exklusive Botox-Party" by two Zahnärzte banned (LG; date unclear). Rabattgutscheine: case law mostly concerns pharmacies and is disputed — [omsels.info § 7 HWG](https://www.omsels.info/die-verbote-oder-was-darf-ich-nicht/2-heilmittelwerbegesetz/dzuwendungen-7-hwg); [Wettbewerbszentrale MedR 2026](https://www.wettbewerbszentrale.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MedR_2026_893ff.pdf); [Rose & Partner](https://www.rosepartner.de/verbotene-werbung-werbeverbot.html).
- **§ 10 HWG:** no public advertising of prescription drugs (Botox by name) — secondary source [dominikvonfalkenhausen.de](https://dominikvonfalkenhausen.de/wissen/welche-marketingagenturen-sind-auf-kampagnen-fuer-faltenunterspritzungen-und-botox-spezial).
- **Meta policy:** ads for cosmetic procedures/surgeries must target 18+. Before/after depictions of general cosmetic procedures are listed as allowed (Meta's own page). Appearance-shaming language and negative self-perception lead to rejection — [Meta Transparency Center – Health & Wellness](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness); [Meta – Cosmetic procedures and wellness](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/content-specific-restrictions/cosmetic-procedures-and-wellness). A July 2026 changelog claims a restructuring of the category (unconfirmed) — [conductatlas](https://conductatlas.com/change/2026-07-23-meta-ads-meta-advertising-policies-3936/). Third-party guides contradict each other on before/after — [gethookd](https://www.gethookd.ai/learn/facebook-ads-for-plastic-surgeons-best-examples-policy-guidelines/); [auditsocials 2026](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-beauty-cosmetics-ads-2026-before-after-photo-rules-appearance-claims-policy).
- **Account bans:** no documented rule disabling an account after a single rejected cosmetic ad was found. Only general "account restrictions" are mentioned — [relevantaudience](https://www.relevantaudience.com/facebook-ads/facebook-ad-banned-words-meta-policy/).
- **No-shows:** see section 3 (US: 4–5 % for existing customers; up to 40 % for poorly qualified leads in an anecdote).
- **Türkei competition:** Haartransplantation Türkei 2,500–5,500 € all-inclusive vs DE about 6,000 € for 2,000 grafts. Brustvergrößerung in the Türkei is listed on the same portal for comparison (Türkei prices not read) — [Mallorca Magazin](https://www.mallorcamagazin.com/nachrichten/leben-und-stil/2026/08/05/155655/haartransplantation-turkei-kosten-2026-der-ehrliche-preisvergleich.html); [123.clinic Türkei](https://www.123.clinic/de/klinik-suche/brustvergrosserung/turkei).
- **Abmahnung practice:** Verbraucherzentrale NRW first sent an Abmahnung and then filed for an injunction — [Brandi PM 01.08.2025](https://www.brandi.net/fileadmin/redakteure/news/pressemitteilungen/2025/PM_BRANDI_250801_Verbraucherzentrale_NRW_Aesthetify_end.pdf).

### Inferences
- The "free/low-threshold anchor offer" in the agency model must be HWG-compliant. A "kostenlose Beratung/Hautanalyse" is likely unproblematic (inference). Discount vouchers, Gewinnspiele and giveaways for treatments are legally risky. Before/after is not available as a hook. This differs fundamentally from US med-spa playbooks.
- The agency is not liable itself for HWG violations in the client's ads, but bears reputation and churn risk. A legal check of the creatives (Fachanwalt Medizinrecht) is advisable.
- Price-driven offers ("ab 129 €") attract price-sensitive leads with low loyalty and compete directly with chains and Kleinanzeigen providers. Higher-quality positioning (doctor as brand, natural results, consultation) is probably more sustainable.

### Gaps
- No concrete documented cases of Meta ad-account bans for German aesthetics practices found.
- No German data on the quality or conversion of Schnäppchen leads compared with consultation leads.

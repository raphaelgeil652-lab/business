# Agenturmarkt & Wettbewerb: Performance-Marketing / Lead-Gen für Beauty, Ästhetik, Zahnästhetik, Kosmetik & Wellness (DE, DACH sekundär), Stand Nov 2026

> Method note (read first): Research was done 20 Nov 2026 via web search only. **Direct page fetching was blocked in this session** (agency sites like panda-marketing.at, dr-leads.de, patienten-plus.de and hello-beauty.at did not resolve), so most facts come from search-result snippets and summaries, not full-page reads. **The Meta Ad Library spot check could not be done** (facebook.com was not reachable from this environment). Treat all agency numbers as self-reported marketing claims unless noted.

## 1. Which agencies specialize in each sub-niche: offers, prices, guarantees, claimed results

### Takeaway
Every target sub-niche has visible specialist agencies in DACH. Kosmetikstudios (Panda Marketing, Hello Beauty, Iconic Leads, Beauty Business 24, WeFlow and others) and Zahnarztpraxen (DoktorLeads/Dr-Leads, docleads, Booked Out Dentist and others) are the most crowded. Ästhetik/plastic surgery has several premium players (Lumus, Junc, Patienten-Plus, Elkeon, Curia, aesthetik-marketing.de). Heilpraktiker, Massage and Breathwork have almost no dedicated performance agencies; the visible offers there are generalist "Praxismarketing" or SEO. Agencies almost never publish prices. The few public figures: Dr-Leads from 2.500 €/month for dental, and Heilpraktiker marketing "im mittleren dreistelligen Bereich pro Monat".

### Inferences
- **Saturation ranking (estimate):** Zahnarzt ≈ Kosmetikstudio (most crowded) > Ästhetik/Schönheitschirurgie (fewer but more professional and premium players) > Haartransplantation (few DE specialists; competition is mainly Turkish clinics doing their own marketing) > Heilpraktiker > Massage/Breathwork (essentially unserved, likely because budgets are low).
- The agency's model (money-back if no leads in 30 days, retainer only after the first won customer) is very similar to Panda's and Iconic Leads' "Erfolgsgarantie" positioning in Kosmetik, so it is **not differentiated** there. In dental, guarantee-style messaging is common in agency pitches, but practices cannot pass it on to patients.
- Agencies that publish a price (Dr-Leads ≥ 2.500 €/month) sit well above the 999–1.500 € retainer. That suggests room in dental and aesthetics to price higher, or to undercut on price.

### Gaps
- No published setup or onboarding fees, pay-per-appointment rates, or contract terms for Panda, Iconic Leads, Hello Beauty, Patienten-Plus or Lumus. Pages could not be fetched. A manual check is needed (Erstgespräch / mystery shopping).
- I could not confirm whether these firms are "Agentur-Programm" or coaching graduates (for example from DACH agency-coaching programs). No source linked them.
- No specialist found for Breathwork or Massagestudio. I could not verify whether one exists.

## 2. Pay-per-lead / pay-per-appointment market prices (proxy for profitability)

### Takeaway
No public German lead marketplace with per-lead prices for Implantate, Haartransplantation or Laser was found. Available data points: **beauty and Kosmetik Meta leads cost roughly 8–12 € in good cases and up to about 50–75 €**, dental and implant leads cost **about 34 € (DE case) to 150–400 USD (US full-arch)**, and an affiliate program for cosmetic surgery pays only **5 € per Anfrage** (up to 100 € only on a sale). Patient ticket sizes are large: Haartransplantation about 7.000 €, Botox 350–600 €, Hyaluron 400–900 €. This supports high affordable CPLs in medical-aesthetic niches.

### Cited Findings
- **Cosmetic-OP partner program:** **5 € per Anfrage**, up to **100 € on purchase** within 180 days. Leads not reachable after 3 attempts are not paid. Partners must make ads HWG-compliant. — [100partnerprogramme.de](https://www.100partnerprogramme.de/p/cosmetic-op-/)
- **Profitprime (Ästhetik):** 277 Terminanfragen at **Ø 7,97 €**. — [profitprime.nl/de](https://profitprime.nl/de/medizinische-kliniken)
- **Kosmetikstudio case (Vienna):** a treatment video plus Instagram lead form produced 62 Anfragen in 4 weeks at a **CPL of 9–12 €**. About one third booked. This is a freelancer's own case. — [lishchuk.com](https://lishchuk.com/blog/meta-ads-freelancer-wien.html)
- **German Meta CPL benchmarks (all industries, 2026):** median about **76 €**, ranging roughly 21–123 € across the year according to one source. Another source puts Meta Lead Ads at about **25 €**. — [marschfahrt.de](https://marschfahrt.de/blog/meta-ads-kosten-facebook-instagram-werbung); [artico.agency](https://www.artico.agency/blog-beitrag/was-kostet-ein-lead-2026); [dorotheeprobst.com](https://www.dorotheeprobst.com/insights/meta-ads-kosten)
- **Beauty & Personal Care Meta CPL (US, June 2026):** median **$51.42**, CPC $3.06. — [cleverzebo.com](https://cleverzebo.com/benchmarks/beauty/meta/)
- **Dental:** Dr-Leads case at **34 €/Lead** (DE, around 2025). — [dr-leads.de](https://dr-leads.de/). US Facebook dental lead about **$76.71** (CPC $9.78). — [adsuploader.com](https://adsuploader.com/de/blog/facebook-ads-by-industry). Full-arch implant leads via Google at **$150–400** (vendor, not methodical). — [dentalmarketingdynamite.com](https://dentalmarketingdynamite.com/dental-implant-leads-cost/). Cost per booked implant consult **$180–340** (vendor claim). — [via search on delmain.co](https://delmain.co/blog/get-more-dental-implant-patients/)
- **Haartransplantation patient value:** Germany averages about **7.371 €** for 2.500 grafts (about 2,93 €/graft). — [medihair](https://medihair.com/de/haartransplantation-deutschland/). docleads gives about 7.000 €. — [docleads.de](https://docleads.de/wie-viel-sollten-aerzte-in-praxismarketing-investieren/). Turkey costs $2.000–4.500 vs Germany $6.000–10.000. — [via Profitprime/search](https://profitprime.nl/de/medizinische-kliniken). A Turkish Meta campaign showed **62 leads at about ₺19 per lead** (very cheap). — [profitprime.nl/de](https://profitprime.nl/de/medizinische-kliniken). Clinic show-up benchmark: plan for **75–90 %** of booked consultations to take place. — [beancount.io 2026](https://beancount.io/de/blog/2026/09/22/hair-transplant-restoration-clinic-bookkeeping-per-graft-pricing-fue-guide)
- **Ästhetik ticket sizes:** Botox **350–600 €**, Hyaluron **400–900 €**. Botox lasts 3–5 months and Hyaluron 6–12 months, which drives repeat visits. Botox led the DGÄPC 2025 statistic at 13,8 %. — [drweb.de](https://www.drweb.de/aesthetische-privatkliniken-geschaeftsmodell/)
- **Implant ticket (DE example):** DentNet fixed price **1.600 €** for implant plus crown, minus the Kassenfestzuschuss. This is a low-price network. — [dentnet.de](https://www.dentnet.de/leistungen/zahnimplantate)
- **Affordable-CPL logic:** example of 30 € per inquiry being affordable at an 8.000 € booking value with 5 % overall conversion. — [via search, ledistagency/scaleee](https://ledistagency.de/blog/leadgenerierung-agentur-kosten)

### Inferences
- **Estimate:** the economic headroom per lead is highest in Haartransplantation (about 7.000 €), Implantate/Veneers (several thousand €) and Schönheitschirurgie. Next come Botox/Hyaluron (moderate ticket but recurring), then Kosmetik treatments (100–300 € per session, with packages up to about 1.000 €, estimated). Heilpraktiker, Massage and Breathwork are the lowest.
- The 5 €/Anfrage affiliate rate in cosmetic surgery shows that raw, unqualified inquiries are cheap to buy. Value sits in qualification and show-up, which supports pay-per-appointment rather than pay-per-lead framing.

### Gaps
- No German lead-marketplace price lists were found (for example per-lead prices on Implantat or Haartransplantation comparison portals). They likely exist but are B2B and not published.
- No German pay-per-appointment prices were found in any niche.

## 3. Churn and client dissatisfaction: why beauty clients cancel

### Takeaway
Hard churn data is absent. Qualitative signals point to dissatisfaction with **long lock-in contracts (12-month packages) sold via high-pressure cold calls** with "today-only" offers and no test phase. Kosmetik owners also traditionally rely on **referrals rather than new-client acquisition**. Trustpilot profiles of the visible Kosmetik agencies are positive but small (n ≈ 5–38).

### Cited Findings
- Hello Beauty (itself an agency selling 12-month packages, so self-interested) describes Kosmetikerinnen under pressure from **aggressive agencies**. It cites cold-call sales of **annual subscriptions** with offers that "expire during the call", lack of transparency, and **no success guarantee or test option**. Its advice is to test with a small project first. — [hello-beauty.at blog](https://hello-beauty.at/kunden-um-jeden-preis-kosmetikerinnen-unter-druck-aggressiver-werbeagenturen/)
- Hello Beauty's own 365 package has a **12-month minimum term**. — [hello-beauty.at](https://hello-beauty.at/)
- **VCP Kabinenkosmetik study 2022** (1.800 Kosmetikerinnen, 1.300 consumers): new-client acquisition "steht weniger im Vordergrund"; studios rely strongly on personal referrals. Only **9 %** of studios use a customer-satisfaction program. — [beauty.de PDF 2022](https://www.beauty.de/cgi-bin/md_beauty/lib/pub/object/downloadfile.cgi/Studienergebnisse_Kosmetik_2022.pdf?oid=49917&lang=1&ticket=g_u_e_s_t)
- In the Heilpraktiker forum (2026 thread), practitioners report marketing service prices ranging from **200 € one-off to 9.800 €**. One user saw **no difference after stopping Google Ads**. — [heilpraktiker-foren.de](https://www.heilpraktiker-foren.de/threads/marketing-werbung-f%C3%BCr-hpp-in-2026-%E2%80%93-was-funktioniert.30420/)
- A Heilpraktiker guide calls social ads for Heilpraktiker, Coaches and Therapeuten mostly "nicht zielführend" because people rarely book directly from an ad. — [via search, wolkenbrecher.de / dumke.biz](https://wolkenbrecher.de/mehr-klienten-gewinnen-als-heilpraktiker/)
- Warning signs listed by a Praxismarketing provider: promises like "Top-Platzierung in 30 Tagen", no initial analysis, opaque prices. — [medizinio.de](https://medizinio.de/?p=33511)
- Generic lead-quality issue in implants: "Implant leads but no consults" is a recognized failure mode, where leads do not turn into consultations. — [webnostra.pro](https://webnostra.pro/en/blog/implant-leads-no-consults/)
- Panda Marketing on Trustpilot: 4.8 from 38 (DE). No cancellation-related reviews surfaced in the snippets. — [Trustpilot DE](https://de.trustpilot.com/review/panda-marketing.at)

### Inferences
- Likely churn drivers (inferred, not measured): (1) leads that do not show up or book, especially with cheap Meta lead forms and "Probebehandlung" offers that attract discount-hunters; (2) the studio cannot handle follow-up calls; (3) the retainer is high relative to Kosmetik margins once the initial wave passes; (4) distrust from previous cold-call agency experiences.
- **Estimate:** 3–6-month average client lifetime for small Kosmetik clients on month-to-month retainers is plausible, based on typical DACH agency-coaching talk, but **no source was found**. The writer should flag this as unverified.

### Gaps
- No Trustpilot or ProvenExpert data with negative reviews or cancellation reasons for any named agency.
- No reddit or gruender.de threads on beauty-agency churn surfaced in the searches.
- No quantified churn or retention rates.

## 4. Which sub-niches are called oversaturated vs underserved

### Takeaway
I found no direct quote from agency owners such as "Kosmetikstudios sind überlaufen". The indirect evidence (many look-alike guarantee agencies, competitor posts about aggressive agency calls, a 2025 court ruling banning cold calls to dental practices) points to **Kosmetik and Zahnarzt as the most saturated with agency outreach**. **Heilpraktiker, Massage and Breathwork are underserved but low-budget and HWG-constrained.** **Haartransplantation in Germany** appears relatively thin on German specialist agencies.

### Cited Findings
- **BVerwG ruling (Az. 6 C 3.23):** unsolicited cold calls to dental practices (in that case Zahngold buyers) are unlawful under DSGVO in conjunction with UWG; there is no presumed consent. Sources disagree on the date: 29.01.2025 per the Medizinrecht-Blog, 29.01.2026 per another source. The volume of calls practices receive is what motivated the litigation. — [zm-online.de](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/ungebetene-werbeanrufe-bei-zahnarztpraxen-auch-zum-ankauf-von-zahngold-sind-rechtswidrig); [medizinrecht-blog.de](https://medizinrecht-blog.de/datenschutz/cold-calls-kaltakquise-im-gesundheitswesen-in-arztpraxen-und-kliniken-erlaubt/); [kanzlei-kotz.de](https://www.kanzlei-kotz.de/kaltakquise-telefonwerbung-bverwg-zieht-klare-grenzen-fuer-werbeanrufe-bei-unternehmen/)
- Competitor complaints about "aggressive Werbeagenturen" targeting Kosmetikerinnen (see Q3). — [hello-beauty.at](https://hello-beauty.at/kunden-um-jeden-preis-kosmetikerinnen-unter-druck-aggressiver-werbeagenturen/)
- Kosmetik blog: "In einem gesättigten Markt ist die richtige Positionierung entscheidend". This refers to the studio market itself, not agencies. — [cloudweb.ch](https://www.cloudweb.ch/marketing-fuer-kosmetik/)
- **Regulatory pressure on Ästhetik ads:** BGH 31.07.2025 (I ZR 170/24, "Dr. Rick & Dr. Nick"/Aesthetify) bans **Vorher-Nachher-Bilder** for Hyaluron/Hyaluronidase nose and chin shaping under § 11 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 1 HWG, including on social media. The case was brought by Verbraucherzentrale NRW ("Faktencheck Gesundheitswerbung"). Medizinio notes that Botox and Hyaluron injections count as operative plastisch-chirurgische Eingriffe under § 11 HWG. — [aerzteblatt.de](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/bundesgerichtshof-untersagt-werbung-fur-hyaluronspritzen-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern-9682baf7-8727-478a-9535-5bb619b29bbe); [lto.de](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/izr17024-bgh-vorher-nachher-bildern-schoenheitsoperation-dr-rick-und-nick-aesthetify); [verbraucherzentrale.nrw](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/urteilsdatenbank/gesundheit-pflege/werbung-mit-vorhernachherbildern-bei-unterspritzungen-100036); [LÄKH 03/2026](https://www.laekh.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Heftarchiv/Einzelartikel/2026/03_2026/Recht_Keine_Werbung_Hyaluron_Injektionen.pdf)
- **Heilpraktiker:** HWG applies, and violations of the Irreführungsverbot can bring fines **up to 50.000 €**. — [medizinio.de](https://medizinio.de/digitalisierung/praxismarketing/heilpraktiker)

### Inferences
- **Least crowded but still profitable (estimate):** (a) **Haartransplantation, DE clinics** (high ticket of about 7.000 €, few German specialists, but competition from Turkish clinics and a small number of clinics, so a small TAM). (b) **Zahnästhetik niche offers** such as Veneers/Aligner via smaller practices, as distinct from general "Zahnarzt-Marketing", which is crowded. (c) **Kosmetikstudios with medical-grade devices** (Laser, Kryolipolyse, HydraFacial) positioned on device ROI rather than as generic Kosmetik, since general Kosmetik is crowded. Botox/Hyaluron is profitable but legally risky for funnel-style creatives (Vorher-Nachher, "Nur für die ersten 20" price offers); doctors are cautious.
- Heilpraktiker, Massage and Breathwork are underserved for a reason: low ability to pay (see Q5), lower willingness to book from ads, and HWG limits for Heilpraktiker. **Estimate:** a 999–1.500 € retainer is likely above what most of them can sustain.

### Gaps
- No direct agency-owner statements (YouTube, LinkedIn, reddit) on niche saturation were found in the searches.

## 5. Ability to pay: typical monthly marketing budgets

### Takeaway
Aesthetic surgery and established dental practices budget **5–10 % of revenue** (for example about 24.000 €/year in one Ästhetik example; agency fees of 2.500 €+/month are common in dental). Kosmetikstudios spend **about 400–1.000 €/month** on ads. Heilpraktiker start at **150–500 €/month**, with 300–600 € as a starter level and 800–2.000 € for established practices.

### Cited Findings
- **Plastische/ästhetische Chirurgie:** benchmark of **5–10 % of target annual revenue**; one example at 8 % gives **24.000 €/year** (about 2.000 €/month). — [dominikvonfalkenhausen.de](https://dominikvonfalkenhausen.de/wissen/welche-kriterien-entscheiden-ueber-das-budget-beim-praxismarketing-fuer-plastische-und-aes)
- **Zahnarztpraxen:** often **5–10 %** of annual revenue recommended; another source says 4–7 % for practices in general. docleads cites an SME average of **3,5 %**, which equals about 7.700 €/year for a practice with 220.000 € Honorarumsatz. Only about **20 %** of practices plan a marketing budget (Stiftung Gesundheit). — [docleads.de](https://docleads.de/wie-viel-sollten-aerzte-in-praxismarketing-investieren/); [bookedoutdentist.de](https://www.bookedoutdentist.de/zahnarzt-marketing/)
- Dental agency pricing **from 2.500 €/month** (Dr-Leads). — [dr-leads.de](https://dr-leads.de/)
- **Kosmetikstudios:** paid Google ads **400–1.000 €/month** (Gaube consulting deck, 2025). — [Gaube PDF](https://gaube-kosmetik.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Beauty-Business-Consulting.pdf). A Vienna freelancer recommends **500–800 €/month** for Meta tests and **1.000–1.500 €** for stable campaigns. — [lishchuk.com](https://lishchuk.com/blog/meta-ads-freelancer-wien.html)

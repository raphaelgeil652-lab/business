## 1. HWG core rules and the BGH ruling on Hyaluron/Botox before-and-after images (I ZR 170/24)

### Takeaway
BGH, Urteil vom 31.07.2025 – I ZR 170/24 ("Dr. Rick & Dr. Nick"/Aesthetify) confirmed that filler injections (Hyaluronsäure/Hyaluronidase) that change the shape of the face count as "operative plastisch-chirurgische Eingriffe" under § 1 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 lit. c HWG. That means the Vorher-Nachher ban in § 11 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 1 HWG applies to them in advertising to the general public, including on Instagram and on websites. The prevailing commentary extends this to Botox and similar minimally invasive treatments. OLG Frankfurt (06.11.2025, 6 U 40/25) added that Instagram Stories which show "before" and "after" in sequence are also covered.

### Cited Findings
- **BGH I ZR 170/24, 31.07.2025:** The BGH dismissed the Revision against OLG Hamm. Before-and-after advertising for filler injections that change the shape of the nose or chin is prohibited. The injections count as operative plastic-surgical procedures under the HWG — [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtsprechung/bgh-izr17024-influencer-aesthetify-vergleichsbilder-werbung-hyaluron-dr-rick-dr-nick-2025-07-31); [BGH Terminhinweis I ZR 170/24 (Verhandlung 3.7.2025, Verkündung 31.7.2025)](https://www.bundesgerichtshof.de/SharedDocs/Termine/DE/Termine/IZR170-24.html); [Verbraucherzentrale NRW press release](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/pressemitteilungen/erfolg-gegen-rick-nick-bgh-verbietet-vorhernachherbilder-bei-unterspritzungen-109545)
- ECLI: DE:BGH:2025:310725UIZR170.24.0. Lower court: OLG Hamm, I-4 UKl 2/24, judgment of 29.08.2024. Some sources date it 28.08.2024 — [ferner-alsdorf](https://www.ferner-alsdorf.de/vorher-nachher-werbung-fuer-hyaluronbehandlungen-unzulaessig/); [vzbv](https://www.vzbv.de/urteile/zur-werbung-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern-fuer-plastisch-chirurgische-eingriffe)
- Legal basis: § 1 Abs. 1 Nr. 2 lit. c HWG (operative plastic-surgical procedures that change the body without medical necessity) combined with § 11 Abs. 1 S. 3 Nr. 1 HWG (no comparative before/after depiction outside Fachkreise) — [mandati.de](https://mandati.de/vorher-nachher-bilder-fuer-schoenheitsbehandlungen-verboten-bgh-bestaetigt-weites-werbeverbot-nach-dem-hwg/); [rosepartner](https://www.rosepartner.de/blog/bgh-zu-verbotener-werbung-im-medizinrecht.html)
- Reasoning reported by secondary sources:
  - It does not matter whether the result is permanent. Hyaluron being broken down by the body does not take the treatment out of "operativ" (OLG Hamm reasoning, upheld).
  - Commentators summarise the test as "Maßgeblich ist das Risiko des Eingriffs, nicht die Invasivität."
  - The court did not need to decide the defendant's comparison to piercing or tattooing.
  - Sources: [aufrecht.de (Terhaag)](https://www.aufrecht.de/nachrichten/wettbewerbsrecht/bgh-urteil-zur-werbung-fuer-botox-und-hyaluronbehandlungen-vorher-nachher-bilder-bleiben-unzulaessig); [horizont](https://www.horizont.net/marketing/nachrichten/urteil-gegen-dr.-rick-und-dr.-nick-unternehmen-duerfen-fuer-kleine-schoenheitseingriffe-nicht-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern-werben-229490); [meyer-koering](https://www.meyer-koering.de/meldungen/vorher-nachher-bilder-auf-instagram-bgh-haelt-solche-werbemassnahmen-fuer-aesthetische-schoenheitseingriffe-fuer-unzulaessig/)
- **Scope:** Advertising for injections as such is not banned. Only the before/after comparison is banned — [verbraucherzentrale.nrw](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/urteile/bgh-verbietet-werbung-mit-vorhernachherbildern-bei-unterspritzungen-100036)
- **Botox:** Commentary says the ruling also covers Botox, but under the "herrschende Meinung" rather than an express holding (the case concerned Hyaluron/Hyaluronidase) — [aufrecht.de](https://www.aufrecht.de/nachrichten/wettbewerbsrecht/bgh-urteil-zur-werbung-fuer-botox-und-hyaluronbehandlungen-vorher-nachher-bilder-bleiben-unzulaessig); [avantcore](https://avantcore.de/vorher-nachher-werbung-hyaluron-botox-verbot-bgh-2025)
- **Who is bound:** The ruling binds everyone who advertises such treatments: doctors, Heilpraktiker, cosmetic businesses and anyone who publicly advertises filler or Botox offers. The HWG regulates the advertising, not a profession — [pixelrobots/HWG-Guide (secondary)](https://pixelrobots.de/blog/heilpraktiker-werbung-hwg-guide/)
- **OLG Frankfurt a.M., 6 U 40/25, 06.11.2025:**
  - A plastic surgeon was barred from Instagram Stories showing a patient before and after removal of a Höckernase.
  - The ban applies even if the images are posted neither directly side by side nor directly one after the other.
  - The judgment was not final at the time of reporting (Nichtzulassungsbeschwerde possible). Press release Nr. 63 of 10.11.2025.
  - Sources: [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/daily/meldung/detail/olg-frankfurt-310o524-schoenheits-op-vergleichende-werbung-heilmittelwerbegesetz-werbeverbot-instagram-story); [Rödl](https://www.roedl.com/insights/olg-frankfurt-a-m-story-sequenzen-koennen-verbotene-vorher-nachher-werbung-sein/); [otto-schmidt](https://www.otto-schmidt.de/news/wirtschaftsrecht/unzulassige-vergleichende-werbung-in-form-einer-instagram-story-fur-einen-medizinisch-nicht-indizierten-eingriff-2025-11-10.html)
- A Bundestag Drucksache (21/4870) is reported to note that influencers keep showing before/after comparisons in video form. I could not open the document itself — [search summary of dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/048/2104870.pdf](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/048/2104870.pdf)
- **Dental treatments:** Practice guides say before/after images for non-operative dental treatments (Bleaching, Veneers, Aligner) are generally allowed since the 2012 HWG reform. They cite OLG Celle, 30.05.2013, 13 U 160/12 (before/after allowed where there is a medical indication). The same guide warns that dentists still get Abmahnungen — [medivendis](https://www.medivendis.de/insights/zahnarzt-vorher-nachher-bilder-erlaubt); [iww](https://www.iww.de/zp/recht/wettbewerbsrecht-werbung-fuer-bleaching-zulaessigkeit-und-grenzen-f132839)
- **Medical indication exception:** Before/after images remain lawful where the treatment is medically indicated, or in an individual consultation (not advertising) — [patientify](https://patientify.io/ratgeber/fotodokumentation-vorher-nachher-recht); [OLG Celle via medivendis](https://www.medivendis.de/insights/zahnarzt-vorher-nachher-bilder-erlaubt)
- **§ 3 HWG (Irreführung):** Misleading advertising is banned for all procedures and treatments to which therapeutic effects are attributed. This includes creating the impression that success is certain (§ 3 S. 2 Nr. 2 lit. a HWG) — [lxgesetze § 3 HWG](https://lxgesetze.de/hwg/3); [aerztezeitung](https://www.aerztezeitung.de/Wirtschaft/Zuviel-heisse-Luft-provoziert-Abmahnung-235153.html)
- **Aligner:** OLG Frankfurt, 27.02.2020, 6 U 219/19: an orthodontist's claim that an aligner system gives "perfekte Zähne" breached the HWG. "Perfect" was not treated as a mere value judgement — [medivendis (search summary)](https://www.medivendis.de/zahnheilkunde)

### Inferences
- Before/after applicability per treatment, inferred from BGH I ZR 170/24 and its "risk not invasiveness" logic:
  - Clearly covered: Schönheits-OPs, Hyaluron filler.
  - Very likely covered: Botox (cosmetic), Fadenlifting, fat-dissolving injections (Lipolyse-Spritze).
  - Probably covered: laser procedures that change the skin or body shape without medical indication (e.g. ablative/fractional resurfacing). This is an inference, not a ruling.
  - Uncertain: Microneedling, especially with needle depth or PRP.
  - Lower risk: Bleaching and Aligner (non-operative, practice guides say allowed). Veneers involve grinding healthy tooth structure, so classifying them as "operativ" is debatable. Treat Veneers as elevated risk.
  - Lower HWG risk, but UWG/§ 3 HWG still apply: pure cosmetic services (Kosmetikstudio facial, laser hair removal).
- Agency rule of thumb: for any injection or OP client, no before/after in ads, Reels, Stories, carousels or landing pages, including "Transformation"/split-screen videos and sequential Stories (OLG Frankfurt).
- § 12 HWG (no advertising outside Fachkreise for listed serious diseases, e.g. cancer and addictions under the Anlage) matters mainly for Heilpraktiker. Not verified in this session.

### Gaps
- Full text of I ZR 170/24 not read (WebFetch blocked). Whether the BGH explicitly addressed Botox could not be confirmed.
- No court ruling found that expressly addresses before/after for Microneedling, Laser or cosmetic-studio treatments.
- Fine levels: under HWG §§ 14/15, criminal liability attaches to § 3 violations, and Ordnungswidrigkeit fines reportedly reach up to 50.000 €. This is from background knowledge and was not verified with a source in this session.

## 2. Gewinnspiele, Verlosungen, Rabatte, Gutscheine, Influencer/Testimonials (§ 7, § 11 Nr. 11/13 HWG)

### Takeaway
- **Gewinnspiele/Verlosungen:** Outside Fachkreise, these are banned for operative-aesthetic procedures under § 11 Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 13 HWG. Per the BGH line, this now includes injections.
- **Flat discounts and fixed prices:** Courts treat these as unlawful for doctors because of mandatory GOÄ/GOZ billing (see section 3).
- **Influencers:** Influencer testimonials for OPs and injections hit both the before/after ban and the § 11 Nr. 11 HWG rules on Äußerungen Dritter, plus the separate duty to label advertising.

### Cited Findings
- **BGH, 18.11.2021, I ZR 214/18 ("Gewinnspielwerbung II"):** § 11 Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 13 HWG is a Marktverhaltensregel under § 3a UWG. A commentary site states that § 11 Nr. 13 also applies to operative plastic-surgical procedures (Schönheitsoperationen) — [omsels.info § 11 Nr. 13](https://www.omsels.info/die-verbote-oder-was-darf-ich-nicht/2-heilmittelwerbegesetz/11-hwg-werbung-ausserhalb-der-fachkreise/nr-13-preisausschreiben-und-gewinnspiele)
- The Kammergericht barred a radio station from raffling Schönheitsoperationen (§ 7 HWG). The BGH referral decision I ZR 214/18 (20.02.2020) discussed how § 11 Nr. 13 relates to § 7 HWG — [omsels.info § 7 Zuwendungen](https://www.omsels.info/die-verbote-oder-was-darf-ich-nicht/2-heilmittelwerbegesetz/dzuwendungen-7-hwg); [rws-verlag](https://rws-verlag.de/aktuell/wirtschaftsrecht-aktuell/bgh-beschluss-vom-20-februar-2020-i-zr-21418-63548)
- A Fachanwalt article on Faltenunterspritzung states that the HWG bans cover all media, including Instagram and other social media — [LÄK Hessen PDF 10/2024](https://www.laekh.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Heftarchiv/Einzelartikel/2024/10_2024/Recht_Werbung_Faltenunterspritzung.pdf)
- **Dentist raffle:** The BVerfG dealt with a dentist who raffled Bleaching treatments at a trade fair. The BVerfG has also accepted advertising with treatment vouchers in some contexts, but the sources give details only peripherally — [iww](https://www.iww.de/zp/recht/wettbewerbsrecht-werbung-fuer-bleaching-zulaessigkeit-und-grenzen-f132839)
- **Rabatte – LG Berlin II, 27.08.2025:** A "20 % Rabatt auf Hyaluronbehandlungen" for clients who had booked Botox was held anti-competitive. GOÄ fees must be set case by case, and individual fee agreements (§ 2 GOÄ) do not justify a blanket advertised discount — [Kanzlei Dr. Bahr](https://www.dr-bahr.com/news/werbung-20-rabatt-auf-hyaluronbehandlungen-ist-wettbewerbswidrig.html); [ip-blogger](https://ip-blogger.de/blog/wettbewerb-und-werbung/lg-berlin-werbung-20-rabatt-auf-hyaluronbehandlungen-ist-wettbewerbswidrig.html)
- **Gutschein auf Rabattplattform – LG Köln:** A doctor's flat-price voucher (€499) on a discount platform was held misleading. The GOÄ provides for billing within a fee range, not fixed prices — [Wettbewerbszentrale](https://www.wettbewerbszentrale.de/landgericht-koeln-haelt-arztwerbung-fuer-wertgutschein-auf-einer-rabatt-plattform-fuer-irrefuehrend/)
- **Exception – OLG Köln, 6 U 285/19:** A Groupon *Wertgutschein* was not misleading where it was expressly stated that the voucher value is credited toward the total fee — [search summary incl. cmshs-bloggt](https://www.cmshs-bloggt.de/gewerblicher-rechtsschutz/wettbewerbsrecht-uwg/pauschale-rabatte-fuer-aerztliche-behandlungen/); [medizinrecht-blog](https://medizinrecht-blog.de/praxisstrategie/werbung-rabatt-arzt/)
- **Influencer labelling:** Separately from the HWG, the Wettbewerbszentrale acted in 11 cases since autumn 2023 against missing or insufficient advertising labels by influencers — [horizont](https://www.horizont.net/marketing/nachrichten/kennzeichnungspflicht-wettbewerbszentrale-greift-bei-influencer-werbung-durch-218546); [lebensmittelzeitung](https://www.lebensmittelzeitung.net/politik/nachrichten/unzureichende-werbekennzeichnung-wettbewerbszentrale-mahnt-influencer-ab-176614)
- The Aesthetify case itself was an influencer-doctor Instagram case (VZ NRW Abmahnung, then lawsuit after the Unterlassungserklärung was refused) — [Brandi PM 01.08.2025](https://www.brandi.net/fileadmin/redakteure/news/pressemitteilungen/2025/PM_BRANDI_250801_Verbraucherzentrale_NRW_Aesthetify_end.pdf)

### Inferences
- For Ästhetik and Schönheitschirurgie clients, agencies should not run "Gewinne eine Lippenunterspritzung" giveaways, "Verlosung", "Model gesucht – kostenlose Behandlung gegen Fotos" campaigns, or flat "20 % auf Botox" or "Botox ab 199 € inkl. aller Kosten" offers.
- Lower-risk alternatives:
  - Wertgutschein with an explicit credit clause (per OLG Köln).
  - Information about the GOÄ-based price range.
  - "Kostenloses Erstgespräch": no court source found. It is generally treated as a permissible information offer, but the risk under § 7 HWG Zuwendungen is not verified.
- Patient testimonials and UGC for OPs and injections: § 11 Abs. 1 S. 1 Nr. 11 HWG bans Äußerungen Dritter where they are made in an abusive, repellent or misleading way. Combined with the § 11 Abs. 1 S. 3 rules for aesthetic OPs, influencer reviews of injections are high-risk. The exact statutory wording of Nr. 11 was not verified in this session.
- For Kosmetikstudios (non-medical services), Gewinnspiele and Rabatte are generally only bound by UWG transparency rules, not GOÄ/HWG. The HWG applies, however, as soon as health effects are claimed.

### Gaps
- No ruling found on a Botox/Hyaluron raffle on Instagram specifically.
- I could not verify whether LG Berlin II (27.08.2025) is final, or the exact Aktenzeichen of the LG Hamburg fixed-price ruling.
- No 2025/2026 Abmahnung statistics from the Wettbewerbszentrale were found. An older trade report says it objected to before/after images for non-indicated treatments in 13 cases in 2024, 11 of them resolved with an Unterlassungserklärung. That figure came from a search summary and the original report was not located.

## 3. Berufsrecht: § 27 MBO-Ä, Zahnärzte, Preiswerbung, GOÄ/GOZ billing obligation

### Takeaway
GOÄ and GOZ are mandatory price law, and they also apply to purely aesthetic medical services. Courts therefore generally hold advertised Festpreise, Pauschalpreise and flat discounts for Botox, Hyaluron, Bleaching or Zahnreinigung to be misleading or anti-competitive. Ranges or "ab"-prices tied to GOÄ/GOZ, and Wertgutscheine with a credit clause, are lower risk.

### Cited Findings
- The KG Berlin held that aesthetic treatments fall within the GOÄ. Scope depends only on whether the service is a professional medical service — [lhr-law](https://www.lhr-law.de/thema/werbung-fuer-schoenheitsbehandlungen/); [medizinrecht-blog](https://medizinrecht-blog.de/praxisstrategie/werbung-rabatt-arzt/)
- The KG Berlin also ordered a doctor to stop advertising Botox for unapproved indications (bunny lines, excessive sweating) — [lhr-law (search summary)](https://www.lhr-law.de/thema/werbung-fuer-schoenheitsbehandlungen/)
- LG Hamburg: an individual fee agreement does not justify general fixed-price advertising aimed at all potential patients — [datenschutz-rv / IT-Kanzlei Lutz](https://datenschutz-rv.de/lg-hamburg-wann-werbung-mit-festpreisen-medienlogos-und-auszeichnungen-unzulaessig-ist/)
- **Zahnärzte – OLG Frankfurt a.M., 21.07.2016, 6 U 136/15:**
  - Advertising PZR and Bleaching at a Festpreis via a deal voucher was unlawful.
  - Bleaching is a Verlangensleistung under § 1 Abs. 2 GOZ that must still be billed under the GOZ.
  - Sources: [kanzlei.biz](https://www.kanzlei.biz/zahnarzt-darf-bleaching-und-zahnreinigung-nicht-zu-festpreis-anbieten-olg-frankfurt-a-m-21-07-2016-6-u-136-15/); [online-und-recht](https://www.online-und-recht.de/urteile/wettbewerbswidrige-werbung-mit-zahnreinigung-und-bleaching-zum-festpreis-oberlandesgericht-frankfurt_am-urteil-v-21-07-2016-6-u-136-15/)
- **Contrary view – VG Münster, 5 K 4424/17:** Minimum prices ("ab") for Bleaching were allowed, because costs for this non-necessary Verlangensleistung cannot be read directly from the GOZ — [iww](https://www.iww.de/zp/recht/wettbewerbsrecht-werbung-fuer-bleaching-zulaessigkeit-und-grenzen-f132839)
- GOÄ billing practice for Botox/Hyaluron (codes, analog billing) is described by a practice-management provider — [medemie](https://medemie.de/en/blog/botox-hyaluron-goae-abrechnung/)

### Inferences
- § 27 MBO-Ä and the state Berufsordnungen allow sachliche, berufsbezogene Information and ban "berufswidrige", i.e. anpreisende, irreführende or vergleichende, advertising. This is from background knowledge, not fetched in this session. Combined with the case law above, lead ads for doctors and dentists should be informational: treatment explanation, the qualified doctor, a consultation CTA.
- A conservative pattern for price advertising: "Preis nach GOÄ, abhängig vom Aufwand; Beratung kostenlos/unverbindlich". "Ab X €" for Bleaching is arguable (VG Münster) but contested (OLG Frankfurt).
- Kosmetikstudios and Massagestudios are not bound by the GOÄ, so fixed prices and discounts are fine there under the UWG (Preisangabenverordnung, genuine "Statt"-prices).

### Gaps
- Text of § 27 MBO-Ä and the Zahnärzte-MBO was not retrieved (gesetze-im-internet/BÄK not fetched). Ärztekammer sanctions (berufsgerichtliche Verfahren) were not researched.
- The new GOÄ reform: status as of late 2026 not researched.

## 4. Heilpraktiker, Breathwork, Wellness-Massagen: health claims

### Takeaway
The HWG covers all "Verfahren und Behandlungen" to which health effects are attributed, so it covers Heilpraktiker, Breathwork and massage ads as soon as they claim to relieve or heal anything. Heilversprechen ("Schmerzen weg", guaranteed success), unproven efficacy claims and ads for diseases listed in § 12 HWG are the main risks. Pure wellness wording ("Entspannung", "Wohlbefinden") is the safe zone.

### Cited Findings
- The HWG applies to procedures and treatments, not just drugs. § 3 HWG prohibits misleading advertising for all procedures and treatments to which therapeutic effects are attributed — [lxgesetze § 3 HWG](https://lxgesetze.de/hwg/3); [Händlerbund](https://www.haendlerbund.de/de/ratgeber/recht/3881-heilmittelwerbegesetz?hsLang=en)
- A Heilpraktiker breached § 3 S. 2 Nr. 2 lit. a HWG by creating the impression that success is certain — [aerztezeitung "Zuviel heiße Luft provoziert Abmahnung"](https://www.aerztezeitung.de/Wirtschaft/Zuviel-heisse-Luft-provoziert-Abmahnung-235153.html)
- OLG Düsseldorf, 20.02.2025, I-20 U 53/24 considered whether "Meine Schmerzen sind einfach weg" in a Heilpraktiker ad is an unlawful Heilversprechen. The outcome could not be confirmed from the search results — [pixelrobots HWG-Guide 2026 (secondary)](https://pixelrobots.de/blog/heilpraktiker-werbung-hwg-guide/)
- OLG München line (as summarised): advertising counts as health-related unless the average consumer reads it as vague "Wellness" puffery — [ra-plutte gesundheitsbezogene Werbung (search summary)](https://www.ra-plutte.de/gesundheitsbezogene-werbung/)
- LG Köln stopped unsubstantiated health claims for an oxygen therapy marketed as a "Heilmittel" — [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/werbung-gesundheit-sauerstofftherapie.html)
- A coaching-law guide addresses Heilversprechen limits for coaches, relevant to Breathwork — [onwalt](https://www.onwalt.de/akademie/heilversprechen-coaching)

### Inferences
- **Breathwork:** Avoid claims like "heilt Angststörungen/Trauma/Asthma", "senkt Blutdruck", "Burnout-Therapie". Use experience and wellness language instead ("Atemreise", "zur Ruhe kommen"). If the provider is not a Heilpraktiker or doctor, therapy claims also risk the HeilprG question (unauthorised Heilkunde), which was not researched here.
- **Massagestudios:** "Wellnessmassage" with relaxation wording is low risk. Claims about curing back pain or lymphatic "Entgiftung" trigger the HWG/UWG.
- Heilpraktiker are not bound by MBO-Ä but are bound by the HWG, including the before/after ban if they offer injections, plus § 12 HWG.

### Gaps
- No Breathwork-specific ruling or Abmahnung was found.
- The § 12 HWG Anlage disease list was not verified in this session.

## 5. Laser and the NiSV

### Takeaway
Since 31.12.2020 the NiSV applies to cosmetic uses of laser, IPL, RF, ultrasound and related devices:
- **Ärztevorbehalt:** Tattoo and permanent-make-up removal by laser and certain ablative or skin-penetrating treatments are reserved to (specialist-trained) doctors.
- **Fachkunde:** Since 31.12.2021, cosmetic studios need certified Fachkunde for laser and IPL hair removal.

### Cited Findings
- Laser and intense light sources may only be operated by people with proven Fachkunde since 31.12.2021. Fachkunde comes from appropriate training or education, and training providers must now be certified — [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/frist-fuer-den-nisv-fachkundenachweis-laeuft-am-3112-ab); [Handwerksblatt certified trainings](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/themen-specials/arbeitsschutz-im-betrieb/kosmetiker-nisv-schulungen-muessen-nun-zertifiziert-sein)
- Laser tattoo removal has been reserved to doctors (not Heilpraktiker or cosmetic businesses) since 31.12.2020. VG Düsseldorf confirmed the Ärztevorbehalt is compatible with higher-ranking law — [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/daily/meldung/detail/vg-duesseldorf-keine-laser-tattooentfernung-durch-heilpraktiker); [rechtslupe](https://www.rechtslupe.de/wirtschaftsrecht/tattooentfernung-per-laserbehandlung-aber-nicht-durch-heilpraktiker-3223411); [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/kosmetiker-duerfen-tattoos-nicht-mehr-mit-dem-laser-entfernen)
- The BMUV overview of cosmetic NiSV applications, including those reserved to doctors with specific Weiterbildung — [BMUV](https://www.bundesumweltministerium.de/?id=5565); [Bayern Gewerbeaufsicht Merkblatt NiSV 2021](https://www.gewerbeaufsicht.bayern.de/gefahrenschutz/doc/merkblatt_nisv_2021.pdf)

### Inferences
- An agency should check that a Kosmetikstudio client advertising laser hair removal holds a NiSV-Fachkunde certificate. It should never run ads for laser tattoo removal, Laser-Resurfacing or Microneedling-with-RF for non-doctors where the NiSV reserves them to doctors. Ads for an unlawful service are misleading under the UWG (§ 5) and expose the client to Gewerbeaufsicht action.
- Microneedling itself (mechanical, without energy devices) is not NiSV. Whether deep needling counts as Heilkunde (Heilpraktiker/doctor only) is contested and not verified here.

### Gaps
- An exact list of NiSV Anlage 3 Arztvorbehalt procedures was not retrieved. A planned NiSV amendment (a 2023/24 Referentenentwurf is referenced in BMUV stakeholder papers) was not confirmed as adopted.

## 6. Meta Advertising Standards (Health & Wellness, cosmetic procedures) and health-data restrictions

### Takeaway
- **Ad policy:** Meta's Health & Wellness ad standard reportedly changed on 22/23 July 2026 from product-based to claims-based enforcement. Several sources say before/after images of general cosmetic procedures are now allowed for 18+ audiences. Negative self-perception and "attacks" on appearance remain banned, and weight-loss/anti-aging comparisons remain restricted. Sources disagree and the official page was not read in full.
- **Health data:** Separately, since early 2025 Meta classifies health and wellness advertisers into restriction tiers that limit lower-funnel events (Purchase/Lead) and custom events from the Pixel/CAPI. On-platform Instant Forms (lead ads) are the common workaround.
- **German law still binds:** Even where Meta allows before/after, the German HWG ban still applies to injections and OPs.

### Cited Findings
- Meta Transparency Center, Health and Wellness ad standard (official page; the snapshot shows a change log entry in July 2026) — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/)
- The July 2026 rewrite, from product-based to claims-based. The date is reported as July 22 by most blogs, July 23 on the official snapshot — [oney.studio](https://www.oney.studio/post/meta-july-2026-health-wellness-ad-policy-update); [clikim](https://clikim.com/meta-health-wellness-policy-update/)

- **Health-data tiers:** Meta classifies sites and apps as handling health data through automated classification and manual review, and assigns them restriction tiers. Sources disagree on which tier wellness falls into and on the timing (February 2025 vs. EU enforcement from September 2025) — [ablecdp](https://www.ablecdp.com/blog/meta-healthcare-restrictions); [wetracked](https://www.wetracked.io/de/post/meta-health-and-wellness-restrictions); [searchengineland](https://searchengineland.com/meta-ads-restrictions-health-wellness-campaigns-453094); [triplewhale](https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/meta-health-and-wellness-brands.md)

### Inferences
- Typical Meta rejection and ban triggers in this niche:
  - "Du"-addressing of perceived flaws ("Deine Tränensäcke…", "Schluss mit Doppelkinn")
  - zoomed body-part close-ups
  - before/after for wrinkle treatments
  - under-18 audiences
  - naming Rx products (Botox)
  - repeated disapprovals, which escalate to account restrictions
- For German clients, the German HWG is stricter than Meta for injections and OPs. Meta's 2026 loosening does not make before/after lawful in Germany.

## 7. Google Ads Healthcare & Medicines policy for Germany

### Takeaway
- **Rx terms:** Google lists Botox/botulinum toxin (also Dysport, Xeomin) as prescription drugs. Promoting Rx drugs in ads or on landing pages in Germany is restricted.
- **Personalised ads:** Cosmetic surgery and injections fall under Google's "health in personalized advertising" sensitive category. Customer Match, remarketing and lookalike audiences are not allowed for them.
- **Practical upshot:** Use "Faltenbehandlung", "Muskelrelaxans-Behandlung" or "Anti-Falten-Behandlung" instead of brand names.

### Cited Findings
- Google Healthcare & medicines policy, with location-specific rules for prescription drugs — [Google Ads policy 176031](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031?hl=en)
- **Prescription drug services:** Google's Rx list (non-exhaustive) includes botox and botulinum toxin, Dysport and Xeomin — [Google Ads policy 15598647](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15598647?hl=en-GB)
- **Conflicting Google pages:** One location table says Rx drugs cannot be promoted in ads or landing pages in Germany. Another lists Germany among locations where certified advertisers may bid on Rx keywords — [Google 176031](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031?hl=en-AU); [Google pharmaceutical manufacturers 15597836](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15597836?hl=en)
- **German HWG layer:** Rx drugs may not be advertised to the public under German law (§ 10 HWG — background knowledge, not fetched), which aligns with Google's restriction.
- **Personalised advertising:** Google's sensitive category "health" includes "invasive medical procedures, including cosmetic surgery, surgical procedures, or injections". Advertisers cannot use customer lists, website-visitor segments or lookalikes for it — [search summary of Google personalized advertising policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/176031?hl=en); [accelerateddigitalmedia](https://www.accelerateddigitalmedia.com/insights/health-policies-and-restrictions-guide-for-google-ads-microsoft-ads-2026/)

### Inferences
- Search ads for Ästhetik should target generic keywords and use neutral copy. Do not use "Botox" in ad text or on landing-page headlines. Bidding on the keyword "Botox" may also trigger disapproval in Germany. Ad accounts with repeated healthcare violations risk suspension.
- Remarketing for clinic visitors is not available. Rely on contextual and keyword targeting and on Performance Max with caution.

### Gaps
- The exact current Germany rule (whether certified Rx keyword bidding is allowed) is contradictory across Google pages. Read the live policy with the location selector set to Germany.

## 8. DSGVO: health data in lead forms (Art. 9)

### Takeaway
- **Lead form data:** Data entered in a lead form for an aesthetic, medical or Heilpraktiker treatment, such as the desired treatment or complaints, is likely health data. EuGH C-21/23 "Lindenapotheke" read the term broadly.
- **Consent:** Processing needs explicit consent under Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. a DSGVO. BGH I ZR 223/19 (27.03.2025) also lets competitors pursue such breaches under the UWG (§ 3a).

### Cited Findings
- **BGH, 27.03.2025, I ZR 223/19 ("Arzneimittelbestelldaten II"):** order data are health data that require explicit consent. Breaches are actionable as Marktverhaltensregel under § 3a UWG — [hv-bayern PDF](https://www.hv-bayern.de/wAssets/downloads/newsletter/TopNews/2026/04/i-zr-223-19a.pdf); [IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/bgh-online-verkauf-arzneimittel-einwilligung-erforderlich.html)
- **EuGH C-21/23 (Lindenapotheke, 04.10.2024):** broad concept of health data. Data entered when ordering pharmacy-only products count as health data — [Loschelder newsletter Oct 2024](https://loschelder.de/fileadmin/redaktion/newsletter/pdf/datenschutzrecht/2024_10_Oktober_2_Beitrag.pdf)
- When data are health data — [FPS Law 2024](https://www.fps-law.de/sites/default/files/publication/ping-2024-02-wann-sind-daten-gesundheitsdaten.pdf)
- A German court ruling that Meta tracking tech violates the GDPR was reported in July 2025. The court and Aktenzeichen were not verified — [Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/blog/german-court-rules-meta-s-tracking-tech-violates-gdpr)

### Inferences
- Lead form minimisation:
  - Ask for name, contact details, preferred time and a coarse interest category.
  - Avoid free-text symptoms, medical history, medications and photos.
  - If you ask for the treatment wish or health information, get explicit, separate Art. 9 consent with information on recipients (agency, CRM, Meta).
  - Do not pass treatment-specific parameters through the Pixel or CAPI (e.g. event names like "Lead_Botox", or URLs with treatment names).
- After BGH I ZR 223/19, competitors can send Abmahnungen for DSGVO health-data breaches, not just the data protection authorities.

### Gaps
- No specific ruling or DPA decision on Meta Pixel or lead forms for German aesthetic practices was found.

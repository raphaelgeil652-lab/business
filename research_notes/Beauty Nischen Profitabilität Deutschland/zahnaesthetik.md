# Zahnästhetik (Bleaching, Aligner, Veneers, Implantate/"Feste Zähne an einem Tag"): Lead-Gen via Meta & Google Ads in Deutschland

Research date: 2026-11-20. Method note: In this session WebFetch could not reach almost any host (facebook.com, zm-online.de, zwp-online.info, dentaly.org, delmain.co, webtonic.io, adlibrary.com, adsuploader.com all failed to resolve: they are not in the session's allowed network domains). ## 1. Which ads run in Germany, recurring patterns (offers, hooks, lead forms vs landing pages, longevity)

### Takeaway
A direct Meta Ad Library scan was not possible (facebook.com blocked in this environment), so concrete advertiser lists and ad longevity are missing. What can be documented: the market uses free scan/consultation anchors (aligners), price anchors for Bleaching (legally contested for dentists), and "feste Zähne an einem Tag" type promises for implants that are flagged as compliance-risky. Lead quality from Meta is clearly weaker than from search.

### Cited Findings
- Aligner brand bestsmile advertises that a dentist/orthodontist performs a findings exam and 3D scan, and calls this appointment free ("kostenloser" Termin/Scan) — this is the "kostenloser 3D-Scan" anchor in the aligner niche — [search summary, Meta Werbebibliothek query](https://www.dentaly.org/de/?p=14914) (manufacturer claim, not independently verified)
- A German guide on Facebook Ads for dental practices flags wording like "feste Zähne an einem Tag" without qualification as risky (compliance), i.e. the hook is common but needs careful qualification — [adsuploader.com: Facebook Ads für Zahnarztpraxen](https://adsuploader.com/de/blog/facebook-ads-dental)
- Example hook pattern in implant ads: contrasting "one day" with a conventional process of 10+ months — [adsuploader.com](https://adsuploader.com/de/blog/facebook-ads-dental)
- Lead quality by channel (2024 benchmark, likely US-based, cited in same guide): Facebook delivered 23.1% of marketing leads, but only 36.2% of them booked an appointment vs 48.2% for paid search and 69.7% for organic leads — [adsuploader.com](https://adsuploader.com/de/blog/facebook-ads-dental)
- Price-anchor ads for Bleaching by dentists exist and are litigated: a dentist advertised Bleaching "99 statt 499 EUR"; the Berufsgericht Münster deemed this berufswidrige Werbung — [IWW](https://www.iww.de/pa/recht/aktuelle-rechtsprechung-verwaltungsgericht-muenster-die-werbung-mit-festpreisen-fuer-das-bleaching-ist-zulaessig-f113164); the VG Münster in contrast held that advertising with Bleaching price examples (Festpreise) is permissible because Bleaching is cosmetic, not medically necessary — [zm-online: Bleaching-Werbung mit Preisbeispielen ist gestattet](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/bleaching-werbung-mit-preisbeispielen-ist-gestattet); [IWW VG Münster](https://www.iww.de/ppz/steuern-und-recht/aktuelle-rechtsprechung-vg-muenster-werbung-mit-festpreisen-fuer-das-bleaching-ist-zulaessig-n112860)
- A Zahnärztekammer imposed a Rüge and 500 EUR Ordnungsgeld on a dentist for Bleaching price advertising — [ZWP 2018: Preisgestaltung und Werbung beim Bleaching](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/preisgestaltung-und-werbung-beim-bleaching-und-kein-ende-in-sicht)
- Groupon-style voucher deals for dental services (incl. Bleaching) were judged anpreisend/unzulässig (LG Köln 2012, per trade press); Zahnärztekammer Nordrhein warned members against Groupon discount campaigns — [ZWP 2012](https://www.zwp-online.info/de/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/zahnarztrecht-werbung-auf-groupon-doch-unzulaessig); [ZÄK Nordrhein Mitgliederinfo Groupon](https://www.zahnaerztekammernordrhein.de/wp-content/uploads/zahnaerzte/berufsrecht-rechtsvorschriften/mitgliederinformationen/ZAEK_NR_MI_Groupon_gesamt.pdf)
- A Berufsgericht für Heilberufe reprimanded a dentist for aligner advertising with emotional slogans promoting a specific aligner brand (product advertising tied to dental services is in principle impermissible per the report) — [ZWP e-paper](https://epaper.zwp-online.info/epaper/11384/export-article/40)
- Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg examined aligner providers and criticised misleading advertising — [vzhh.de](https://www.vzhh.de/themen/gesundheit-patientenschutz/aligner-das-geschaeft-den-schoenen-zaehnen)
- Bleaching providers outside dental practices market "peroxidfrei"/"rechtssicher" gels (e.g. pearlux) — [pearlux FAQ](https://www.pearlux.de/faq/peroxidfrei-vs-peroxid); "Dental Beauty Studio"-type businesses publish "kosmetisches Bleaching oder Zahnarzt?" comparison content — [dentalbeautystudio.de](https://www.dentalbeautystudio.de/blog/kosmetisches-bleaching-oder-zahnarzt/)

### Inferences
- Pattern likely in market (consistent with the above, not directly observed in Ad Library): Aligner = "kostenloser Scan/Beratung" + Ratenzahlung; Implantate = "feste Zähne an einem Tag"/"All-on-4" + kostenlose Erstberatung/Implantat-Check, often with Meta Instant Forms; Bleaching = price anchors (which for dentists sit in a legal grey zone; Festpreis examples OK per VG Münster, but "statt"-strike-through discounts and vouchers have been sanctioned).
- For an agency, the compliance constraint is a real creative limitation: no anpreisende Werbung, no discount/urgency mechanics for dentist clients; creative must be "sachlich". This narrows the classic "Rabatt + Countdown" lead-gen playbook.
- Meta leads for dental convert to appointments at markedly lower rates than search leads (36% vs 48% in the cited benchmark) → appointment-setting/qualification is the key value lever.

### Gaps
- No direct Meta Ad Library observation (advertiser names, number of active ads, run durations, lead form vs landing page split) — facebook.com was not reachable from this session. Manual check recommended: facebook.com/ads/library, Land DE, terms "Bleaching", "Zahnaufhellung", "Aligner", "Invisalign", "Veneers", "Implantate", "feste Zähne".
- No Google SERP observation of current paid ads/landing pages.
- No confirmed concrete "ab 49 €/Monat" Ratenzahlung ad examples found.

## 2. Typical prices in Germany and Selbstzahler vs Kasse

### Takeaway
All four categories clear the ≥300 € ticket filter; Aligner, Veneers (multi-tooth) and Implantate clear ≥1.000 € comfortably. Bleaching (250–700 €) is borderline. Bleaching, Veneers and adult Aligner are essentially 100% Selbstzahler; Implantate get only a GKV Festzuschuss (~553 € per tooth 2026 without Bonusheft) on the crown portion.

### Cited Findings
- **Bleaching in-office:** 250–700 € per treatment; some dentists charge per tooth 70–150 € — [dentaly.org Bleaching Kosten](https://www.dentaly.org/de/zahnbleaching/bleaching-kosten); Verbraucherzentrale NRW cites ~30–70 € per tooth in-office — [Verbraucherzentrale NRW](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/wissen/gesundheit-pflege/medikamente/bleaching-moeglichkeiten-der-zahnaufhellung-12908) (conflicting ranges)
- **Home bleaching via dentist (Schienen):** 250–400 €; Walking-Bleach (devitaler Zahn) 50–150 € per tooth — [dentaly.org](https://www.dentaly.org/de/zahnbleaching/bleaching-kosten)
- Bleaching is generally a Selbstzahlerleistung (no GKV coverage) — [zahnzusatzversicherung-experten.de](https://www.zahnzusatzversicherung-experten.de/zahnbleaching-kosten-versicherung.html) (updated Nov 2024)
- **Aligner overall:** range 1.290–10.000 € depending on provider/case — [ilovemysmile.de Aligner Kosten 2026](https://ilovemysmile.de/aligner-kosten-was-sie-jetzt-fuer-ihr-traumlaecheln-einplanen-sollten/); at dentist/KFO typically 2.000–6.000 € (Apotheken Umschau 2026 as cited), mostly remote-supervised 1.500–4.000 € (Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg 2024 as cited) — [ilovemysmile.de](https://ilovemysmile.de/aligner-kosten-im-vergleich-so-finden-sie-das-beste-angebot/); [Apotheken Umschau](https://www.apotheken-umschau.de/mein-koerper/aligner-statt-zahnspange-vorteile-kosten-und-risiken-1534871.html)
- **Invisalign:** typically 3.500–6.500 €, minor corrections from 2.500 € — [dentaly.org](https://www.dentaly.org/de/?p=297)
- **DrSmile (DTC/hybrid):** 1.790–4.190 €, care mostly digital — [shapemysmile.de Aligner Kosten Vergleich](https://shapemysmile.de/aligner-kosten-vergleich); low-cost providers 1.700–3.500 € "nur leichte Korrekturen" — [klinikkosten.de](https://www.klinikkosten.de/tabellen/kieferorthopaedie-kostenvergleich/)
- **Kasse Aligner:** GKV does not cover aligner therapy for adults even with KIG indication; adults get GKV KFO coverage only for severe anomalies (KIG 4/5), usually combined KFO-kieferchirurgisch; Zahnzusatzversicherungen often reimburse part — [ilovemysmile.de Versicherung 2026](https://ilovemysmile.de/invisalign-kosten-versicherung-2026-der-ultimative-guide-einer-kieferorthopaedin-zur-erstattung-inkl-muenchener-verein-check/); [zahn-direkt.de](https://www.zahn-direkt.de/ratgeber/zielgruppen/kieferorthopaedie-kosten)
- **Veneers:** 500–2.000 € per tooth; simpler composite/ceramic variants from ~300 €/tooth; no GKV coverage for cosmetic veneers — [dentaly.org](https://www.dentaly.org/de/?p=297); [zahnzusatzversicherung-experten.de](https://www.zahnzusatzversicherung-experten.de/zahnbleaching-kosten-versicherung.html)
- **Einzelimplantat inkl. Aufbau + Krone:** most sources 1.800–3.500 € (other ranges 1.500–3.500 € / 2.000–4.500 €); Knochenaufbau/Sinuslift +300–1.500 € — [deutschland-rechner.de](https://www.deutschland-rechner.de/zahnimplantat-kosten-rechner); [ERGO Ratgeber](https://www.ergo.de/de/Ratgeber/zahngesundheit/zahnersatzkosten); an agency blog cites 2.500–4.000 € incl. Krone — [zahnarztgrowth.de](https://zahnarztgrowth.de/patienten-fuer-implantate)
- **GKV Festzuschuss Implantat:** covers part of the crown (based on Regelversorgung = Brücke), not the implant; 60% of Regelversorgung, up to 75% with Bonusheft; ~553 € (552,96 €) per single tooth in 2026 without Bonusheft; typical Eigenanteil 1.247–2.947 € without Bonusheft/Aufbau — [Allianz Festzuschuss](https://www.allianz.de/gesundheit/zahnzusatzversicherung/festzuschuss-zahnersatz/); [deutschland-rechner.de](https://www.deutschland-rechner.de/zahnimplantat-kosten-rechner)
- **All-on-4 / "Feste Zähne an einem Tag":** 8.000–25.000 € per Kiefer in Germany — [zahnzusatzversicherung-experten.de All-on-4](https://www.zahnzusatzversicherung-experten.de/all-on-4-methode-ablauf-kosten-vorteile-und-nachteile.html); other sources 15.000–25.000 € per Kiefer — [dentaly.org All-on-4 Kosten](https://www.dentaly.org/de/informationen-rund-um-das-zahnimplantat/all-on-4-kosten); foreign competition (Turkey/Hungary) advertised from ~3.000 € per Kiefer (broker claims) — [bookimed](https://de.bookimed.com/clinics/procedure=same-day-all-on-4-dental-implants); [flymedi](https://www.flymedi.com/de/zahnmedizin/all-on-4)
- Affordability pressure: per a Nov 2025 survey report, every 3rd patient has to weigh whether treatment is currently affordable — [ZWP 12.11.2025](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/dental-news/branchenmeldungen/finanzlage-beeinflusst-zahnarztbesuch-jeder-3-patient-muss-abwagen-ob-behandlung-aktuell-leistbar-ist)

### Inferences
- Ticket ranking for the agency's filter: All-on-4 (8–25k € per Kiefer) >> Aligner (2–6k €) ≈ Veneers set (e.g. 6–10 teeth × 500–2.000 € = 3–20k €, estimate) > Einzelimplantat (1,5–3,5k € gross, ~1–3k € Eigenanteil) > Bleaching (250–700 €).
- Bleaching alone is a weak fit (≥300 € borderline, ≥1.000 € fails); it works better as a low-threshold front-end/anchor that feeds Veneers/Aligner upsells within a dentist client.
- Ratenzahlung/financing ("ab X €/Monat") is a structurally important conversion lever because tickets are high and every third patient reports affordability concerns (estimate based on the survey above).

### Gaps
- No 2026 practice price pages fetched directly; no robust survey of typical in-office Bleaching price in 2026.
- No reliable data on share of aesthetic dentistry revenue in total practice revenue (Selbstzahler share).

## 3. Legal limits: who can be a client (Bleaching-Studios vs Zahnärzte), Berufsordnung, HWG

### Takeaway
In Germany Bleaching with >0,1% H2O2 (or H2O2-releasing compounds like Carbamidperoxid) is reserved for dentists (first application in the practice); Bleaching-Studios without a Zahnarzt may only offer ≤0,1% / peroxide-free "cosmetic" whitening, and German chambers and courts treat studio bleaching as Zahnheilkunde ohne Approbation. Studios are therefore a legally fragile, low-ticket client segment. Dentist clients are constrained by the Berufsordnung (sachliche Information erlaubt, anpreisende/irreführende/vergleichende Werbung verboten) and HWG.

### Cited Findings
- EU Directive 2011/84/EU, implemented via KosmetikV: products with >0,1% up to 6% H2O2 may only be sold to dentists; first use must be done by/in the dental practice, then home bleaching possible; ≤0,1% products are freely sold and may be applied without a dentist; H2O2-releasing compounds (Carbamidperoxid, Zinkperoxid) are treated equivalently — [ZWP 2012 Neue Kosmetikverordnung für Bleaching](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/dental-news/branchenmeldungen/neue-kosmetikverordnung-fuer-bleaching); [Spitta Update Zahnaufhellung](https://dentalwelt.spitta.de/zahnmedizin/update-zahnaufhellung/); [LGL Bayern Zahnbleichmittel](https://www.lgl.bayern.de/produkte/kosmetika/kosmetische_mittel/et_zahnbleichmittel.htm)
- Bundeszahnärztekammer position: "Zähnebleichen (Bleaching) ist eine zahnärztliche Leistung"; per § 1 ZHG practising Zahnheilkunde is reserved for approbierte Zahnärzte — [BZÄK Statement](https://www.bzaek.de/service/positionen-statements/einzelansicht/zaehnebleichen-bleaching-ist-eine-zahnaerztliche-leistung.html)
- OLG Frankfurt (01.03.2012, Az. 6 U 264/10): a ZFA running a "Zahnkosmetikinstitut" was prohibited from cleaning teeth and from bleaching with products >6% H2O2 (case brought by LZK Hessen) — [agz-rnk.de summary](https://agz-rnk.de/mundzaehne/behandlungen-mundzaehne/zahnaufhellung-bleaching); [IWW: Zahnbleaching und Airflow nur mit Zahnarzt](https://www.iww.de/aaz/kurz-informiert/aktuelle-rechtsprechung-zahnbleaching-und-airflow-verfahren-nur-mit-zahnarzt-n56108)
- Another decision (per trade press) held that Bleaching and PZR in such a studio constitute Ausübung der Zahnheilkunde ohne Approbation under § 18 ZHG — strafbar even if a dentist was consulted beforehand in the individual case — [agz-rnk.de](https://agz-rnk.de/mundzaehne/behandlungen-mundzaehne/zahnaufhellung-bleaching)
- OVG NRW (2006) and BVerwG (2007) followed the view that PZR, Fluoridierung, Politur, Prophylaxe and Bleaching belong to Zahnheilkunde — [ZWP 2011 berufsrechtliche Aspekte der ästhetischen Zahnheilkunde](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/aktuelle-berufsrechtliche-aspekte-der-aesthetischen-zahnheilkunde)
- Zahnärztekammer Nordrhein (2013 circular): Bleaching-Studios and Bleaching-Lounges "gehören der Vergangenheit an", Zahnaufhellung may only take place in dental practices; Kammer states it acts against Kosmetikstudios — [ZÄK Nordrhein RZB 2013/01](https://www.zahnaerztekammernordrhein.de/wp-content/uploads/zahnaerzte/berufsrecht-rechtsvorschriften/weitere-themen/RZB_2013_01_Zahnaufhellung_KosmetikVO.pdf); [ZÄK Nordrhein statement](https://www.zahnaerztekammernordrhein.de/bereich/presse-publikationen/statements/bleaching-so-geht-die-kammer-gegen-kosmetikstudios-vor/)
- Austria differs: OLG Wien deviated from an OGH decision in favour of Gewerbebetriebe (Kosmetiker); WKO publishes guidance for Zahnbleaching by cosmetics trades — [WKO Zahnbleaching](https://www.wko.at/gewerbe-handwerk/fusspfleger-kosmetiker-masseure/zahnbleaching); [AGES](https://www.ages.at/forschung/wissen-aktuell/detail/wasserstoffperoxid-in-zahnbleichmitteln)
- Not resolved by sources: whether a customer self-applying a ≤0,1% gel in a studio under instruction is lawful; studio vendors claim "peroxidfrei, rechtssicher" (commercial interest) — [pearlux](https://www.pearlux.de/faq/peroxidfrei-vs-peroxid)
- **Berufsordnung:** § 21 Abs. 1 MBO-Z: sachliche Informationen über die Berufstätigkeit erlaubt, berufswidrige Werbung verboten; Landesberufsordnungen name in particular anpreisende, irreführende, herabsetzende oder vergleichende Werbung as berufswidrig — [BZÄK MBO-Kommentar (LZK BW)](https://lzk-bw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Downloads/Recht/3.Berufsrecht/BZAEK_MBO-Kommentar.pdf); [IWW: zahnärztliche Werbung aus berufsrechtlicher Sicht](https://www.iww.de/zp/recht/berufsrecht-zahnaerztliche-werbung-aus-berufsrechtlicher-sicht-was-ist-erlaubt-was-nicht-f75936)
- § 15 MBO-Z (angemessenes Honorar, GOZ) makes Pauschal-/Rabattpreise e.g. for Bleaching critical — [ZWP 2019: Gutscheine, Rabatte und Pauschalpreise für PZR, Bleaching und Co.](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/gutscheine-rabatte-und-pauschalpreise-fuer-pzr-bleaching-und-co); [ZWP 2015 Rabatt, Fixpreis und Co.](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/rabatt-fixpreis-und-co-zahnarztpraxen)
- Werbung is not generally banned for dentists, even if aimed at patient acquisition; decisive is sachlich vs anpreisend — [zm-online 2017: Was ist erlaubt, was nicht](https://www.zm-online.de/archiv/2017/11/praxis/was-ist-erlaubt-was-nicht/)
- A dentist was fined (Geldstrafe) for violating Berufsrecht via advertising (2017 case) — [ZWP 18.10.2017](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/werbung-geldstrafe-wegen-verstoss-gegen-berufsrecht)
- **HWG / Vorher-Nachher:** § 11 Abs. 1 S. 3 HWG bans before/after images for operative plastisch-chirurgische Eingriffe (since 2012 HWG reform, other procedures liberalised); OLG Celle (30.05.2013, 13 U 160/12) held a dentist's before/after images of treatment results did not violate HWG; whether Bleaching/Veneers before-after are allowed remains unsettled in practice — [IWW: Werbung mit Vorher-Nachher-Bildern zulässig](https://www.iww.de/zp/recht/marketing-werbung-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern-zulaessig-f55807); [ZWP 2009](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/keine-werbung-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern)
- BGH 31.07.2025 (I ZR 170/24, "Dr. Rick & Dr. Nick"/aesthetify): before/after advertising for Hyaluron-Unterspritzungen prohibited, broad reading of the ban — aesthetic medicine, not dentistry, but signals strict interpretation — [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/daily/meldung/detail/bgh-izr17024-influencer-aesthetify-vergleichsbilder-werbung-hyaluron-dr-rick-dr-nick)
- **DTC aligners & law:** BZÄK (2017) said DTC providers exceed Heilpraktiker/ZHG limits; LG Kiel found dental cooperation with PlusDental could violate the duty of conscientious practice; zm 2022 test: only PlusDental had initial exam by a dentist — [zm-online: so arbeiten gewerbliche Aligner-Start-ups](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/so-arbeiten-gewerbliche-aligner-start-ups-1); [zm-online: Aligner-Anbieter scheitern erneut vor Gericht](https://www.zm-online.de/artikel/2020/defekte-kompositfuellungen-gute-gruende-fuers-reparieren/aligner-anbieter-scheitern-erneut-vor-gericht-1)

### Inferences
- Bleaching-Studios/Kosmetik: low ticket (cosmetic ≤0,1% treatments are typically priced well below dentist bleaching — estimate, no reliable price source found), active enforcement by Kammern, and Meta ad policy + UWG exposure → poor fit for the ≥300 €/≥1.000 € client filter and a reputational/legal risk for the agency.
- The agency's natural client is a Zahnarzt/Praxis (owner-run), selling Aligner/Veneers/Implantate; Bleaching only as anchor (e.g. "kostenlose Beratung/Zahnaufhellungs-Check" rather than "99 € statt 499 €").
- Creative must avoid: strike-through discounts, vouchers/urgency, superlatives, unqualified "feste Zähne an einem Tag" promises, brand-product promotion (aligner brand), and (cautiously) before/after images.

### Gaps
- No primary text of KosmetikV Anlage / BZÄK statement read in full (fetch blocked).
- No current (2024–2026) court ruling found specifically on Bleaching-Studios using ≤0,1% gels with self-application.
- No source on whether Meta's own health ad policies have caused rejections in DE dental niches.

## 4. CPL, cost per booked consultation, show rate, close rate benchmarks

### Takeaway
German benchmark data is scarce and almost entirely agency self-reported: ~80–150 € per qualified Anfrage and 50–150 € CPA for general new patients; implant Google Ads budgets of 2.000–4.000 €/month for 8–15 implant consultations (≈130–500 € per consultation, derived). US data: implant/Invisalign CPL ~$107–212 (Google, Delmain) up to $180–450, show rate targets 75–90%, cost per acquired implant patient $800–2.000. No reliable consult-to-close benchmark found.

### Cited Findings (DACH)
- A German agency states average cost per qualified Anfrage of 80–150 € (no methodology) — [search summary citing German dental agency pages, e.g. dentalarmy.de](https://dentalarmy.de/); [marketingfürzahnärzte.de](https://xn--marketingfrzahnrzte-uwb21c.de/leistungen)
- German guide: CPA typically 50–150 € per Neupatient; many practices budget 4–10% of annual revenue for marketing — [medizinio Zahnarzt Marketing 2026](https://medizinio.de/?p=34728)

- US agency case studies: 1.090 leads at avg $24.94 CPL (year 2); 633 leads at $37.02 CPL (year 1) — [ranktracker dental case studies](https://www.ranktracker.com/de/blog/dental-marketing-case-studies-guide/)
- Facebook leads: only 36.2% booked appointments vs 48.2% paid search vs 69.7% organic (2024 benchmark) — [adsuploader.com](https://adsuploader.com/de/blog/facebook-ads-dental)
- Multiple sources warn raw lead counts/low CPL are vanity metrics; implant leads require fast follow-up and handling of cost objections — [Dentaltown: Calls, Consults & Cases](https://www.dentaltown.com/channel/1657/calls-consults-cases-dental-growth-beyond-cost-per-lead); [Driven Dental Marketing](https://drivendentalmarketing.com/why-dental-implant-leads-not-converting/)

### Inferences
- Rough unit economics (estimate, assumptions labeled): implant/All-on-4 Meta lead in DE ~50–150 €; with ~35–50% booking and ~70–80% show → ~150–500 € per attended consultation; if 20–35% close (assumption, no source) → ~500–2.000 € ad cost per won implant case. Against tickets of 3.000–25.000 € this is viable; for Bleaching (250–700 €) it is not, unless upsells follow.
- The agency's "retainer only after first won customer" model is attractive in this niche because the first All-on-4/Aligner case alone covers months of retainer, but sales cycles for implants (Heil- und Kostenplan, financing, second opinions) can be weeks to months → time-to-first-win risk.

### Gaps
- No independent German benchmark for CPL/cost per booked consultation/show rate/close rate in dental aesthetics; all DACH figures are agency claims.
- No implant-specific consult-to-case close rate from any credible source.

## 5. Market: number of practices, owner-run vs MVZ/chains, demand trends, ad-market saturation

### Takeaway
Germany has roughly 30.500 Einzelpraxen (2023) plus Berufsausübungsgemeinschaften and ~1.500 Z-MVZ; owner-run practices still dominate numerically, but Einzelpraxen are shrinking (–4.800 since 2015) while MVZ grew from 46 to 1.483. The dental marketing agency market is visibly crowded (many specialised agencies, performance/"erfolgsbasiert" offers), and DTC aligners have retreated (DrSmile sold, left Austria, service problems).

### Cited Findings
- Einzelpraxen fell from 35.305 (2015) to 30.531 (2023); Z-MVZ rose from 46 to 1.483 in the same period (KZBV data) — [ZWP e-paper citing KZBV/BZÄK Statistik](https://epaper.zwp-online.info/epaper/11567/export-article/12)

- Addressable owner-run universe is large (~30.000 Einzelpraxen + BAGs), but the subset doing Implantologie/Aligner/Veneers at scale with Selbstzahler focus is much smaller (estimate: a few thousand; no source).

## 6. Capacity: spare capacity vs overbooked (Fachkräftemangel); appetite for new private patients

### Takeaway
Staffing, not patient demand, is the binding constraint for many practices: 96% of dentists report difficulties hiring ZFA/ZMF/ZMV, only about a third rate their staffing as good, and 43% have already reduced their treatment offering (2024). This cuts both ways: practices are less hungry for generic Kassenpatienten but rationally prefer high-margin Selbstzahler cases (Aligner, Veneers, Implantate) per chair-hour. ZFA trainee numbers are recovering (2025).

### Cited Findings
- 96% of dentists report difficulties finding ZFA/ZMF/ZMV; only about a third of practices rate their staffing as good/very good (Zi/ZäPP survey) — [zm-online 2024: Fehlende Fachkräfte schränken Patientenversorgung ein](https://www.zm-online.de/artikel/2024/zm-2024-20/fehlende-fachkraefte-schraenken-patientenversorgung-ein)
- 43% of practices have already reduced their treatment offering due to staff shortage (2024 survey) — [ZWP 19.09.2024: Umfrage belegt Fachkräftemangel](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/dental-news/branchenmeldungen/umfrage-belegt-fachkraftemangel-wirkt-sich-auf-praxisalltag-aus)
- Bundesagentur für Arbeit 2023 bottleneck analysis ranked ZFA first among most affected professions; ~38,5% of ZFA trainees starting 2023 dropped out early — [ZWP 06.05.2025: Zahnarzt sucht Team und findet keins](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/dental-news/branchenmeldungen/zahnarzt-sucht-team-und-findet-keins-wenn-personalmangel-die-praxis-lahmlegt)
- Turnaround: ZFA training contracts up significantly in 2025; 13.614 new ZFA trainees; ~43% of practices trained apprentices in 2024 — [ZWP 05.12.2025](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/dental-news/branchenmeldungen/13614-neue-zfa-so-boomt-der-drittbeliebteste-ausbildungsberuf); [ZWP 24.10.2025](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/dental-news/branchenmeldungen/zfa-ausbildung-im-aufwartstrend-2025-deutlich-mehr-ausbildungsvertrage); [zm-online 2025: Es kommen wieder mehr junge Fachkräfte](https://www.zm-online.de/news/detail/es-kommen-wieder-mehr-junge-fachkraefte-in-die-zahnarztpraxen)
- No-shows increasing, straining scarce appointment capacity (KZV Land Brandenburg) — [KZVLB PM Terminausfälle](https://www.kzvlb.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Aktuelles/Presse/PM_Terminausf%C3%A4lle_Patientensensibilsierung.pdf)
- Patients (Discover Dental survey, Sept 2025, n=2.000): drivers of satisfaction are friendly team (54%), short waiting times (44%), proximity (39%), fast (online) booking (37%); average 28 days acceptable wait for check-ups — [zm-online 2026: Digitale Angebote ersetzen nicht den menschlichen Eindruck](https://www.zm-online.de/artikel/2026/zm-2026-03/digitale-angebote-ersetzen-nicht-den-menschlichen-eindruck-vor-ort)

### Inferences
- Many practices are capacity-constrained for routine/Kasse work; the sales pitch must be "fewer, higher-value Selbstzahler cases per chair-hour" not "more patients".
- Aligner treatments are relatively chair-time-light (scan + periodic checks) and Bleaching is assistant-delegable → good fit for capacity-constrained practices; All-on-4 needs surgeon + lab capacity but very high ticket.
- Qualification filter for the agency: practices with an owner who does Implantologie/Aligner, an available Beratungs-slot block per week, and ideally a dedicated Patientenkoordinator (to handle lead follow-up; otherwise Meta leads' low booking rates will kill results).
- Rising no-shows support adding confirmation/reminder workflows as part of the agency service.

### Gaps
- No survey on share of practices with Aufnahmestopp for new patients, nor on willingness to take new Privat-/Selbstzahler patients specifically.
- No data on average free chair capacity per practice.

## 2. Top offer types per treatment, plus legal risk (brief)

### Takeaway
- **Laser and PMU are cosmetic or tattoo-like services.** Free trials, packages and model prices are common and legally fairly unproblematic.
- **Botox/Hyaluron is the most restricted:**
  - no before/after (BGH July 2025)
  - free consultations, vouchers and discounts are risky under § 7 HWG
- **Bleaching:**
  - "ab"-prices have been held permissible
  - before/after is risky; the sources conflict and there is new 2025 OLG case law

### Cited Findings
**Laser**
- Autumn and winter are the recommended start time. A dermatologist quoted on apotheken.de says the winter half-year suits treatment better than summer. — [apotheken.de](https://www.apotheken.de/krankheiten/krankheiten-und-therapie/hno-atemwege-und-lunge/9948-dauerhafte-haarentfernung)
- Several sessions about 4 weeks apart are needed, so starting in autumn makes sense if the goal is smooth skin by summer. — [Stylight](https://www.stylight.de/Magazine/Beauty/Dauerhafte-Haarentfernung-Mit-Ipl-Laser/)
- Typically 6–8 sessions over several months, so a discount on session 1 covers only a small part. Reputable providers don't promise complete hairlessness. — [weekend.at](https://www.weekend.at/lifestyle/beauty/laser-haarentfernung-im-faktencheck), [wissenschaft.de](https://wissenschaft.de/artikel/laserhaarentfernung-was-licht-im-haarfollikel-tatsaechlich-bewirkt)
- NiSV: commercial laser hair removal may only be done by people with proven expertise (Fachkunde), refreshed every 5 years. This works as a trust element on the LP. — [Bayer. Gewerbeaufsicht Merkblatt NiSV](https://www.gewerbeaufsicht.bayern.de/gefahrenschutz/doc/merkblatt_nisv_2021.pdf), [Region Hannover NiSV-Erläuterungen](https://www.hannover.de/content/download/1061333/file/Erl%C3%A4uterungen%20zur%20Verordnung%20zum%20Schutz%20vor%20nichtionisierender%20Strahlung%20%28NiSV%29.pdf)

**PMU**
- Typical price ranges:
  - Powder Brows 300–600 € ([desired.de](https://www.desired.de/beauty/powder-brows-alles-was-du-ueber-den-augenbrauen-trend-wissen-musst--01HF6R2KVMDZ44P0NVV26P04RX))
  - AT: brows 300–700 €, eyeliner 150–300 €, lips 250–450 € ([herold.at](https://www.herold.at/blog/permanent-make-up), DACH/AT)
- A touch-up after about 4 weeks "should always be included in the price" (desired.de). One Vienna studio charges it separately at 99 € (AT). — [desired.de](https://www.desired.de/beauty/powder-brows-alles-was-du-ueber-den-augenbrauen-trend-wissen-musst--01HF6R2KVMDZ44P0NVV26P04RX), [herold.at](https://www.herold.at/blog/?p=83776)
- Legal contrast: in the Hyaluron case the BGH explicitly did not follow the doctors' comparison with **permissible** advertising for ear piercing, piercing and tattooing. This implies tattoo-like services such as PMU are not caught by the HWG before/after ban. — [Ärzteblatt BGH](https://www.aerzteblatt.de/news/bundesgerichtshof-untersagt-werbung-fur-hyaluronspritzen-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern-9682baf7-8727-478a-9535-5bb619b29bbe), [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/izr17024-bgh-vorher-nachher-bildern-schoenheitsoperation-dr-rick-und-nick-aesthetify)

**Botox/Hyaluron**
- BGH I ZR 170/24 (31.07.2025, "Dr. Rick & Dr. Nick"/Aesthetify): hyaluron injections count as "operative plastisch-chirurgische Eingriffe" under § 11 Abs. 1 S. 3 HWG. Before/after advertising (website and Instagram) is banned. — [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtsprechung/bgh-izr17024-influencer-aesthetify-vergleichsbilder-werbung-hyaluron-dr-rick-dr-nick-2025-07-31), [Kanzlei Plutte](https://www.ra-plutte.de/bgh-hyaluron-vorher-nachher-werbung-verboten/)
- Per ZDF, minimally invasive face treatments in general are affected. — [ZDFheute](https://www.zdfheute.de/politik/bgh-urteil-werbung-fotos-schoenheitsop-100.html)
- Free consultation as a hook is risky: LG Nürnberg held a free consultation at a "Tag der Schönheit" unlawful under § 7 HWG. A free medical consultation alongside obesity treatment was also classed as a prohibited gift. A Landgericht prohibited a "Botox-Party" ad. — [Wettbewerbszentrale MedR 2026](https://www.wettbewerbszentrale.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MedR_2026_893ff.pdf), [DATEV Adipositas](https://www.datev.de/web/de/berufsgruppenuebergreifend/nachrichten/ndsr/recht/Keine-kostenlose-aerztliche-Beratung-neben-medizinischer-Adipositasbehandlung)
- Package prices and discounts for medical services were deemed unlawful (fee-law reasons, GOÄ), reportedly also for self-pay cosmetic treatments. — [Ärztezeitung](https://www.aerztezeitung.de/Wirtschaft/Aerztliche-Rabatte-koennen-zum-Bumerang-werden-291505.html), summarised in [Wettbewerbszentrale MedR 2026](https://www.wettbewerbszentrale.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MedR_2026_893ff.pdf) (secondary summary, not the ruling text)
- Vouchers: OLG Karlsruhe 9.12.2025 (14 U 49/25) treated a 25 € voucher as a generally unlawful gift under § 7 HWG. **Context: pharmacy and medicines, not aesthetics.** — [MedR 2026](https://www.wettbewerbszentrale.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MedR_2026_893ff.pdf)

**Bleaching**
- VG Münster: fixed prices for bleaching are permissible. Featuring the price visually up front suggests "especially cheap" and was criticised. "ab xy €" was considered permissible. — [iww.de VG Münster](https://www.iww.de/pa/recht/aktuelle-rechtsprechung-vg-muenster-werbung-mit-festpreisen-fuer-das-bleaching-ist-zulaessig-n112855), [zwp-online Gutscheine/Rabatte PZR, Bleaching](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/gutscheine-rabatte-und-pauschalpreise-fuer-pzr-bleaching-und-co)
- Before/after for bleaching is contested:
  - A 2009 article says it was permissible (bleaching is not a plastic-surgical operation). — [zwp 2009](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/keine-werbung-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern)
  - A 2025 trade article says it is not permissible for non-indicated aesthetic procedures. — [zwp 2025 Social Media](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/social-media-in-der-zap-chancen-grenzen-und-praktische-tipps)
  - OLG Frankfurt 6.11.2025 (6 U 40/25): before/after posts on social media for medically non-indicated procedures fall under the ban. It is unclear whether bleaching was the procedure in that case. — [iww.de](https://www.iww.de/zp/recht/heilmittelwerbegesetz-vergleichende-werbung-mit-vorher-nachher-bildern-bleibt-verboten-f170555)
- Dentists are bound by professional law: no "anpreisende" (promotional) advertising. Berufsgericht Münster 19.06.2023 found a dentist's Facebook product ad for aligners to be in breach. — [zwp 2025 Aligner](https://www.zwp-online.info/zwpnews/wirtschaft-und-recht/recht/berufswidrige-und-anpreisende-fremdwerbung-mit-alignersystem)
- Price range for in-office bleaching:
  - 250–700 € ([dentaly](https://www.dentaly.org/de/zahnbleaching/bleaching-kosten))
  - 300–1,000 € (Verbraucherzentrale NRW) ([VZ](https://www.verbraucherzentrale.nrw/wissen/gesundheit-pflege/medikamente/bleaching-moeglichkeiten-der-zahnaufhellung-12908))
  - Not covered by statutory health insurance.

### Inferences
- **Laser:** "Kostenlose Probebehandlung/Testschuss + Hautanalyse" is a proven real offer (HelloHairless). Package prices per zone are common, and autumn/winter is the natural campaign start. Right now (Nov.) is laser season.
- **PMU:** The "Preis inkl. Beratung, Vorzeichnen & Nachbehandlung" bundle is the market standard. Making it explicit in the ad is a low-risk, honest offer. Model appointments are a real portfolio-building tactic for new artists. They don't suit an established studio's main campaign.
- **Botox/Hyaluron:**
  - The ad should not use before/after, free consultation, discount or voucher hooks.
  - What remains: doctor presence (introducing the doctor), information/education ("Wie läuft eine Faltenbehandlung ab?"), "Termin zur ärztlichen Beratung vereinbaren" (without "kostenlos"), and a natural-results message without comparison pictures.
  - Note: "Botox" is a brand name, and prescription-drug advertising to the public (§ 10 HWG) is another risk. This was not researched here and belongs in the legal deep-dive.
- **Bleaching:** Keep a dentist's ad sober and informative: "Zahnaufhellung in der Praxis – ab X € inkl. PZR". The price should not visually dominate. No before/after in social ads until legal clarification.

### Gaps
- No evidence on which offer converts best per treatment in DE. No German A/B data was found.
- No ruling found that directly addresses a Botox voucher or discount in aesthetics. The voucher cases concern pharmacies.

## 3. Creative formats, hooks, seasonality

### Takeaway
I found no German creative-performance data. The platform rules are clear:
- Meta requires 18+ targeting for cosmetic procedures.
- Before/after is barred by Meta for wrinkle/anti-aging procedures.
- For "general cosmetic procedures" Meta's policy listing allows before/after; third-party guides conflict.
- Seasonality is well supported for laser (autumn/winter). For PMU and bleaching it is plausible but unsourced.

### Cited Findings
- **Meta Health & Wellness policy:**
  - Ads for cosmetic procedures must not target under-18s.
  - Wrinkle-treatment/anti-aging procedures may not use before/after or side-by-side images.
  - General cosmetic procedures depicting before/after transformation are listed separately (allowed for 18+).
  - Several third-party sites claim a blanket ban, which conflicts with Meta's text.
  - Sources: [Meta Transparency – Health and Wellness](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness), [Meta – Cosmetic procedures and wellness](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/content-specific-restrictions/cosmetic-procedures-and-wellness), [auditsocials 2026 (third party)](https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/meta-beauty-cosmetics-ads-2026-before-after-photos-appearance-claims-policy)
- **Laser seasonality:** winter half-year preferred, with UV protection before/after sessions. Sun-avoidance windows vary by source (2 weeks before/after vs. up to 8 weeks after). — [apotheken.de](https://www.apotheken.de/krankheiten/krankheiten-und-therapie/hno-atemwege-und-lunge/9948-dauerhafte-haarentfernung), [Stylight](https://www.stylight.de/Magazine/Beauty/Dauerhafte-Haarentfernung-Mit-Ipl-Laser/)
- **Laser prep:** shave only, don't epilate or wax before treatment (the laser needs the hair root). Avoid sun and solarium. — [salonkee](https://salonkee.de/search/hair-removal/escheburg/laser-permanent-hair-removal), [weekend.at](https://www.weekend.at/lifestyle/beauty/laser-haarentfernung-im-faktencheck)
- **PMU:** each PMU needs 2–3 treatments at 4-week intervals (herold.at, AT). — [herold.at](https://www.herold.at/blog/permanent-make-up)

### Inferences (template proposals, not sourced as "proven")
- **Laser offer card (fits the PV template):** studio photo of a device or treatment situation, no B/A needed.
  - Big offer: "Kostenlose Probebehandlung – deine Wunschzone"
  - Sentence: "Jetzt im Herbst starten, im Sommer glatt."
  - Fact line: "Diodenlaser · NiSV-zertifiziert · 6–8 Sitzungen"
  - CTA: "Termin sichern"
  - Video variant: process/ASMR clip of the laser on armpit or leg (no B/A needed, legally unproblematic).
- **PMU:**
  - Here B/A or healed results are the strongest proof and are not affected by HWG (tattoo-like), as long as Meta 18+ is respected.
  - Offer: "Powder Brows inkl. Vorzeichnen & Nachbehandlung – ab X €"
  - Process video: pre-drawing with a ruler/thread, then the reveal.
  - Spring/wedding season is plausible but unsourced.
- **Botox/Hyaluron:**
  - Doctor-to-camera video (who am I, how a consultation works, "natürlich, nicht gemacht" as an attitude, not a promise of results)
  - No B/A, no discount, no "kostenlos"
  - CTA: "Beratungstermin anfragen"
- **Bleaching:**
  - Dentist or team video explaining the process
  - Fact line "in der Praxis · inkl. PZR · ab X €"
  - Price not as the eye-catcher
  - Seasonal angles (wedding, summer, Christmas parties) are plausible but unsourced

### Gaps
- No German hook examples from real ads (Ad Library blocked).
- No evidence for UGC vs. doctor-to-camera vs. static performance in DE.
- No sourced seasonality data for PMU or bleaching.

## 5. Landing-page structure that converts bookings

### Takeaway
- Vendor sources (US) agree:
  - one treatment-specific page per offer (message match)
  - proof (results, credentials, treatment-specific reviews) close to the CTA
  - visible starting prices
  - 5–8 FAQs
  - a specific booking CTA, sticky on mobile
  - explain what happens at the consultation, to reduce no-shows
- German add-ons: NiSV certificate (laser), doctor profile (Botox), dentist (bleaching), no B/A on pages for Botox/Hyaluron (BGH) and probably bleaching.

### Cited Findings
- Treatment-specific landing pages convert at 4–8% vs. 1–2% for generic med-spa pages (vendor data, unclear method). One agency reports 3.2% → 6.7% in 90 days through landing page work alone. — [webtonic LP stats (vendor)](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/medical-spa-landing-page-statistics), [unicornplatform 2026](https://unicornplatform.com/blog/med-spa-websites-in-2026/)
- Treatment-specific reviews, practitioner credentials and real photos belong on the page. One source warns that burying testimonials on a single page means 90% of visitors never see them. — [bestppcfirm LPs (vendor)](https://bestppcfirm.com/blog/medspa-landing-pages-that-book-consultations), [growth99](https://growth99.com/blog/build-a-high-converting-medical-spa-website/)
- 5–8 FAQs per treatment page. Make consultation fees, starting prices, packages and financing understandable; hidden pricing is a common blocker. — [unicornplatform](https://unicornplatform.com/blog/med-spa-websites-in-2026/), [landingdoctors](https://landingdoctors.com/audit-for/med-spa)
- Use specific CTA wording ("Book a Consultation") plus mobile click-to-call and a sticky booking button. Message match between ad and LP is claimed to push conversion above 6%. — [redefineweb](https://redefineweb.com/blog/med-spa-web-design-best-practices-2/), [bestppcfirm](https://bestppcfirm.com/blog/medspa-landing-pages-that-book-consultations)
- Explain the consultation's purpose, duration, preparation and next steps after the form, and track show rate, not just forms. — [leadorigin](https://leadorigin.com/?p=67791)

### Inferences (proposed fixed LP skeleton, same for all four)
1. Hero: one photo, offer headline, one sentence, booking button above the fold
2. Trust strip: Google stars, NiSV / doctor / dentist / PMU certificate
3. "So läuft's ab" in 3 steps (consultation, treatment, follow-up/touch-up)
4. Results:
   - PMU and laser: real healed results
   - Botox/Hyaluron and bleaching: **no B/A**; use a doctor profile and explanation instead
5. Prices/packages: "ab"-prices, what is included
6. FAQ: pain, number of sessions, durability, preparation, downtime
7. Form or booking widget plus a repeated CTA

### Gaps
- No German LP conversion data. All numbers are US vendor claims.

## 6. Benchmarks (CPL / cost per booking)

### Takeaway
- No reliable German CPL per treatment.
- Best broad anchor: WordStream 2025, Beauty & Personal Care at $51.42 CPL on Facebook lead campaigns (US/global).
- Single-case anecdotes: laser studio Prague about $15 CPL; Botox $14–32 CPL (US agency).
- Vendor ranges conflict wildly ($8–25 vs. $75–200).

### Cited Findings
- WordStream 2025: Beauty & Personal Care $51.42 average CPL (Facebook lead campaigns). All-industry CPL rose 21% to $27.66. Google average CPL is $70.11. — [WordStream 2025](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025), [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/facebook-ad-costs-jump-beat-google-461690)
- **Laser studio, Prague (EU, freelancer self-report):** $400/month budget, 26 leads, about $15 CPL, average customer check $120. — [freelancehunt](https://freelancehunt.com/en/showcase/work/target-laser-studio-praga/1892187.html)
- **US med spa (agency case):** 367 Botox leads in 4 months, average about $17 CPL. Botox Lip Flip $14 and general Botox $32 CPL. — [Sagapixel case study](https://sagapixel.com/?p=24942) (could not fetch; via search summary)
- Vendor ranges:
  - $8–25 for non-surgical, Botox/filler $8–18 on Meta (attributed to Scalehaven, unverified) — [webtonic](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/medical-spa-aesthetics-facebook-ads-statistics)
  - $50–100 for "high-performing" and $75–200 average — [prospyrmed](https://prospyrmed.com/blog/post/paid-ad-roi-benchmarks-aesthetic-clinics)
  - These conflict.
- A fitness campaign can show $6 CPL and still lose money. Cost per paying customer is what counts. — [adsuploader](https://adsuploader.com/de/blog/facebook-lead-ads-cost-per-lead)

### Inferences
- A planning assumption of about 20–50 € CPL on Meta for laser/PMU in rural DE seems defensible as a hypothesis between the Prague anecdote and WordStream. **Not sourced, test it.**
- Botox/Hyaluron leads will likely cost more in DE because of the HWG restrictions (weaker hooks).
- Measure cost per **booked and attended** appointment, not CPL.

### Gaps
- No DE/EU cost-per-booking figures for any of the four treatments.
- Nothing at all for bleaching.
